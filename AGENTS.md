## Image direction

Use the approved Discover **graphic-novel / limited-colour screenprint** style for new and replacement editorial illustrations throughout the site. The user selected this direction on 7 October 2026. Voxel, pixel-art and Minecraft-like studies are historical alternatives, not the current direction.

Follow `docs/design/image-direction.md` for the palette, exact shared prompt, reference assets, composition and visual review requirements. Example artwork must show recognizable work connected to the sourced story. Keep instructional schematics and official identification logos accurate; generated artwork represents fictional people and generic equipment.

Present editorial images cleanly, with no green bottom caption strips or illustration/disclaimer bands. Record provenance in design notes and retain accessible alt text. Apply this presentation and the graphic-novel theme to future examples and site artwork.

Teaching illustrations use the same style as static images, with explicit directional arrows replacing animation and live numbered explanations preserving readability. Cap example-page story and teaching images at 680px on desktop; keep their full aspect ratio and fit narrow screens.

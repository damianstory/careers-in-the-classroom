## C1 v1 — proposed classroom card

**Title:** Senior Wildlife Biologist

**Employer:** Canacre

**Employer note:** An environmental and land-services company. This posting describes wildlife assessment for energy-sector projects; it does not establish involvement with Wilder.

**Location:** Calgary, Alberta, Canada

**Arrangement:** Full-time; remote/hybrid arrangement not established here.

**Seniority:** Senior role; the posting asks for 10 or more years of relevant experience.

**Summary:** Study wildlife and habitat so project teams can understand and reduce their effects on animals.

**What they do:**
- Lead wildlife surveys, including surveys for species at risk.
- Assess habitat and recommend ways to reduce impacts during construction.
- Prepare wildlife reports and permit applications using field evidence.

**Skills:** Wildlife survey methods, bird identification, interpreting field data, and technical report writing. The posting lists GIS tools as an asset.

**Connection to the classroom story:** Wilder's story raises questions about what animals need to survive. This role offers a related example of collecting wildlife and habitat evidence to inform decisions about human activity. It does not describe breeding or releasing burrowing owls.

**Displayed status:** Accepting applications when checked 28 Sep 2026.

**Source for job facts:** [Canacre employer posting](https://canacre.applytojob.com/apply/DJX45aEs66/Senior-Wildlife-Biologist).

### Reviewer notes

The pilot inspected substantive duties and an application form on the employer page. The classroom connection is our interpretation. Score: connection 3 (shared wildlife/habitat assessment methods), concrete work 3 (surveys, reports, and mitigation), added value 3 (first proposed wildlife assessment card for this example). The current app expires an open label after 30 days; implementation should recheck that rule and the source.

Proposed role label: wildlife assessment. Resolve the role ID and Wilder story links during an authorized implementation; this packet does not establish that those links exist already.

## C2 v1 — proposed classroom card

**Title:** Intermediate to Senior Vegetation & Wetland Ecologist

**Employer:** Klohn Crippen Berger

**Employer note:** An engineering and environmental consulting company. This posting concerns vegetation and wetlands; it does not establish involvement with Wilder.

**Location:** Calgary, Alberta, Canada

**Arrangement:** Hybrid, full-time; the posting also describes fieldwork and travel throughout Canada.

**Seniority:** Experienced role; the posting asks for six or more years of applicable experience.

**Summary:** Study plants and wetlands to help teams assess project impacts and plan habitat protection or restoration.

**What they do:**
- Plan and carry out vegetation and wetland field surveys.
- Use field observations and imagery to map ecological areas and wetland boundaries.
- Prepare reports that support impact assessment, mitigation, and regulatory decisions.

**Skills:** Plant identification, vegetation and wetland ecology, GIS and data management, and technical report writing.

**Connection to the classroom story:** Animal survival depends on habitat as well as the animals themselves. This role shows one kind of work involved in understanding and managing habitats, using different ecosystems and projects from Wilder's burrowing-owl work.

**Displayed status:** Accepting applications when checked 28 Sep 2026.

**Source for job facts:** [KCB employer posting](https://careers.klohn.com/jobs/8249859-intermediate-to-senior-vegetation-wetland-ecologist).

The pilot inspected duties, the Calgary/hybrid location, and an application cue on the employer page. The classroom connection is our interpretation. Score: connection 2 (adjacent habitat work, not the same species/ecosystem), concrete work 3 (surveys, mapping, and reports), added value 3 (adds plant/habitat work alongside C1's animal focus). This is an editorial judgment; the card should be omitted if it distracts from the intended lesson.

Proposed role label: habitat ecology. Resolve the role ID and Wilder story links during an authorized implementation.

**Employer note:** The lithium-extraction company in this classroom example.

**Location:** Demonstration facility east of Olds and Didsbury, Alberta; not a Calgary worksite.

**Arrangement:** Shift-based field and plant operations; the posting describes a 24-hour demonstration facility.

**Level:** Experienced operations role; relevant field or plant experience is requested.

**Summary:** Operate and monitor the equipment used to demonstrate lithium processing.

**What they do:**

## Coverage, holds and changes

- **E3 employer search:** Apify returned no records, while the employer careers page linked 11 postings. Two were read in full and preserved as PDFs. Zero Actor results would have missed the best featured-employer evidence.
- **Calgary related search:** 15 records included one obvious same-requisition Worley duplicate. Stantec's water EIT role was selected for its early-career contrast. Several manufacturing/rail or general-energy roles were weaker matches; a Victaulic result mixed Calgary and Utah and was not promoted without resolving location. The 15-result query limit means this is a sample.
- **Wilder employer search:** 10 results included food-service, events, facilities and horticulture roles. Animal care best matched this story. Its original ADP page explicitly identifies Wilder, the Strathmore facility, requisition 1178, and a 9 October deadline. Reaching the query limit does not establish that these are all open jobs.
- **GHGSat source refresh:** BambooHR now lists the government role and a Senior Backend Developer role. Workable has three records: English/French backend and English full-stack. The French full-stack version seen yesterday is absent from this feed; this does not mean the English role closed. The backend role on two systems is a likely duplicate, not a new career pathway. Only G1's application flow was directly verified today; the other GHGSat feed entries are not new application-status approvals.
- **Existing GHGSat scientist:** still treated as the previously captured historical example; no new claim that it reopened. The pilot's Onterris/Montrose emissions-technician lead remains on hold; its source/identity issues were not resolved in this run. The source check from yesterday is not relabeled as a fresh check.
- **Geographic breadth:** this baseline prioritized featured employers and local gaps. The manual pilot's historical broader examples remain available; no new nationwide historical sweep was required to fill the five-card shortlist.

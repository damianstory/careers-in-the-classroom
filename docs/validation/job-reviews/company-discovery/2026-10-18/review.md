### CGY-068/v1 — EFW Radiology — 10/10

**Proposed classroom question:** How can an echo help make a picture of something inside the body?

**Exact proposed selection summary:**

EFW provides ultrasound at its Uxborough clinic in Calgary. The equipment uses returning sound waves to help form images. Students could use a simple echo model to connect travel time and distance, then discuss why acquiring a useful image and interpreting it are different parts of the work.

**Company / local work:** Uxborough clinic in Calgary explicitly lists general ultrasound; employer explains the technique. [source 1](https://www.efw.ca/services/general-ultrasound/), [source 2](https://www.efw.ca/locations/uxborough-17/)

These are related learning routes, not verified employer requirements. Admission, registration and intake details belong in full research.

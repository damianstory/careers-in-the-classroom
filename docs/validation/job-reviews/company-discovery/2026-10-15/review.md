These are editorial judgments, not accuracy probabilities. **Decision: approved for full research (user, 15 October 2026).**

## Coverage and limitations

This is an initial purposive discovery pass, not a census. Employer/product/careers pages were the principal verification sources. Calgary Economic Development, Alberta Innovates and industry-association material supplied additional leads/context. Searches covered food/agriculture, water/environment, instruments, aviation, manufacturing/trades, biomanufacturing, geoscience, digital construction, energy and software. This pass does not yet cover all city sectors; future passes should deliberately include construction services, community businesses and non-technology employers.

Screened 20 new company identities against current featured examples, job-review employers and the earlier 20-company sample. No extra rejected leads were evaluated in this pass; existing known companies were excluded before screening. Earlier companies remain known leads in the cumulative index rather than being lost or relabelled new. No current posting inventory or full programme/admissions audit was attempted.

Important identity/location findings: Summit Nanotech redirects to Summit Lithium Technologies, whose current contact page identifies Calgary sorbent R&D and pilot manufacturing. FLYHT identifies itself as an FTG company. Oncolytics lists San Diego headquarters and a Calgary office; local research functions remain unresolved. SensorUp's current contact page did not establish a substantive Calgary worksite. These two held candidates are not ready for full-example approval without clarification.

Some evidence is dated, particularly Avmax's 2024 capabilities and Carbonova's 2025 demonstration announcement. The former supplies work evidence alongside a current company site; the latter does not prove the plant is now commissioned. Employer performance claims were not independently verified or promoted into classroom facts.

Source snapshots, errors and hashes are in source-manifest.json and evidence/. Free search-reader evidence was used where direct capture failed; a successful page fetch is not evidence of a current vacancy. No personal contact enrichment or outreach was performed.

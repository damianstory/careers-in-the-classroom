## 1. Wilder Institute — can helping individual owls help a population recover?

**Local connection:** Wilder Institute is connected to Calgary Zoo; its burrowing owl field program works in southeastern Alberta. Distinguish the Calgary connection from the release locations. [Institute](https://wilderinstitute.org/) · [Program](https://wilderinstitute.org/program/burrowing-owl-program/)

**The work:** The program cares for vulnerable young owls through their first winter, then releases adult pairs and monitors them. This is an example of using an intervention at one life stage to support population recovery. [Program](https://wilderinstitute.org/program/burrowing-owl-program/)

**People and roles:** A July 2025 interview identifies a conservation research population ecologist leading the program. A 2023 account also describes animal care technicians and veterinarians supporting the owls. These are dated role observations, not a verified current staff roster. [Researcher interview](https://wilderinstitute.org/2025/07/21/head-starting-burrowing-owls-for-a-wild-future/) · [Care team account](https://wilderinstitute.org/blog/100th-owl-returns-to-the-prairies/)

**Course connection:** Biology 20 ecosystems and population change; Biology 30 population and community dynamics. [CBE biology topics](https://eps.cbe.ab.ca/biology-20-30.aspx)

**Explain it to students:** Saving animals and restoring a population are connected but different goals. Scientists need evidence about what happens after release, not just a count of animals cared for.

**Discussion prompt:** What would you measure over several years to decide whether this intervention is helping? What comparison would make your conclusion more convincing?

**Pathways to explore:** Ecology, zoology, and environmental science are relevant study directions, not confirmed hiring requirements at Wilder. UCalgary lists these among its science programs. Animal care and veterinary routes warrant separate research rather than assuming the same qualification fits every role. [Program guides](https://science.ucalgary.ca/current-students/undergraduate/program-guides)

**Practitioner conversation:** How do you decide what to measure, and how do field observations change the intervention?

**Mindset illustrated:** Evaluate outcomes beyond the most visible success metric.

## 2. E3 Lithium — how do you separate something valuable from a complex liquid?

**Local connection:** Calgary-based company advancing lithium extraction in Alberta; its Clearwater project is between Calgary and Red Deer. A Calgary address does not mean the extraction site is inside the city. [Company](https://www.e3lithium.ca/)

**The work:** E3 is developing direct lithium extraction from brine. A published process role describes work connecting laboratory and demonstration systems to plant design. This is development and scale-up work, not evidence that the planned commercial facility is already operating. [Role description](https://e3lithium.ca/_resources/careers/Intermediate-Water-Process-Engineer_JobDescription.pdf?v=021812)

**People and roles:** The posting identifies a water process engineer/process chemist role involving process design, testing, equipment evaluation, and optimization. It explicitly connects that role to extraction development. A posting is evidence of work the employer sought, not proof that someone currently occupies it. [Role description](https://e3lithium.ca/_resources/careers/Intermediate-Water-Process-Engineer_JobDescription.pdf?v=021812)

**Course connection:** Chemistry 20 solutions and quantitative chemical change; Chemistry 30 electrochemistry provides a wider battery connection, not a claim about E3's extraction mechanism. [CBE chemistry topics](https://eps.cbe.ab.ca/chemistry-20-30.aspx)

**Explain it to students:** Separating a useful substance is only part of the challenge. A process also has to work repeatedly, at a useful scale, with acceptable resource use and cost.

**Discussion prompt:** If two separation methods recover different amounts of lithium but use different amounts of water and energy, what would you need to compare before choosing one?

**Pathways to explore:** The published role requests a degree in chemical engineering or process chemistry and substantial experience. Treat it as a later-career example, not a first job after school. A separate company biography describes its process and technology director's chemistry and chemical engineering degrees, showing one actual route rather than a universal requirement. [Role requirements](https://e3lithium.ca/_resources/careers/Intermediate-Water-Process-Engineer_JobDescription.pdf?v=021812) · [Management biographies](https://www.e3lithium.ca/corporate/management/)

**Practitioner conversation:** What changes when a process moves from a lab to a larger facility?

**Mindset illustrated:** Optimize across competing constraints rather than one number.

## 3. GHGSat — how can you find an invisible gas from space?

**Local connection:** GHGSat lists a Calgary office and a Montreal headquarters. Roles below refer to the organization as a whole; their location in Calgary has not been established. [Offices and work areas](https://www.ghgsat.com/contact/)

**The work:** Its satellite instruments measure methane; the company describes a spectrometer at the heart of its satellites. This offers a connection between measurement, electromagnetic radiation, and environmental decisions. [Satellite technology](https://www.ghgsat.com/technology/satellite-constellation/)

**People and roles:** The company identifies satellite engineering, atmospheric science, software development, data science, and commercial work as team areas. These are observed role families, not a list of verified vacancies or project assignments. [Work areas](https://www.ghgsat.com/contact/)

**Course connection:** Physics 30 electromagnetic radiation; Science 30 electromagnetic energy and environmental topics; Science 10 global energy systems as a broader introductory connection. [Physics](https://eps.cbe.ab.ca/physics-20-30.aspx) · [Integrated science](https://eps.cbe.ab.ca/science-10-20-30.aspx)

**Explain it to students:** Detecting a gas, estimating how much is escaping, and deciding what to do are different tasks. Instruments, analysis, and people interpreting the results all contribute.

**Discussion prompt:** If a sensor detects no methane, does that prove none is present? What information about the instrument and observation conditions would you want?

**Pathways to explore:** Physics, engineering, and computing/data study are plausible directions for different parts of this work, not confirmed GHGSat hiring requirements. UCalgary's engineering physics program explicitly combines physics and engineering; its program overview identifies space physics among application areas. [Engineering physics](https://schulich.ucalgary.ca/departments-centres/departments-and-programs-overview/engineering-physics) · [Engineering programs](https://schulich.ucalgary.ca/future-students/undergraduate/programs/majors)

**Practitioner conversation:** How do you turn an instrument reading into a conclusion someone can act on?

**Mindset illustrated:** Understand measurement limits before drawing conclusions.

## News sample A — a fresh development at an organization with a Calgary presence

## News sample B — beyond Calgary, relevant to biology

**Published:** 23 September 2026. **Location:** Bay Area lab; no Calgary connection established. **Status:** Organization-reported early research with a linked preprint; independent replication and peer-review status not verified here.

Anthropic announced a life sciences laboratory and reported identifying an enzyme system through AI-assisted analysis followed by human laboratory work. The organization explicitly says the system's function is still unknown. For Biology 30, this connects DNA, proteins, and scientific inquiry. Ask students: what is the difference between noticing an unusual pattern, proposing a function, and demonstrating that function? Do not describe this as a proven new gene-editing tool or treatment. [Research announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system)

## Suggested monthly edition to test

Use one local example, one relevant recent development, and one classroom question. Include a genuine student opportunity only when eligibility and dates are verified; none was researched for this sample. Let teachers switch topics or request a practitioner. Do not pad an edition with weak news, and do not call an older evergreen example a new discovery.

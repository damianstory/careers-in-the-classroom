## What to look at first

The five original standouts remain useful starting points: Botaneco, Nanalysis, Exergy, Livestock Water Recycling and Avmax. For a contrasting second group, Northern RNA, Foremost, Carbonova, Eguana and FTG add biological production, trades, materials, energy and software work.

Review these as full text drafts, with the marked limitations retained. **Exact Alberta course/outcome badges are not yet verified**; subject links are proposed, not asserted as approved curriculum alignment. Nanalysis and VEERUM particularly need care to avoid forcing specialist work into a weak course match. Images are described in production briefs, not generated. These remain explicit implementation gates, not silent promises that every draft is publication-ready.

## What the research changed

The Summit, VEERUM and Eguana headline questions have been narrowed from their discovery proposals to match the researched stories. Their original proposal approvals remain unchanged; these revised full-copy headlines are pending review.

- Avmax’s current website directly establishes Calgary maintenance services; the draft no longer depends solely on a dated brochure.
- Botaneco’s recovered technology page supports water-based separation of oilseed components. Broad sustainability and health marketing claims are omitted.
- Summit’s current site distinguishes Calgary sorbent R&D/pilot manufacturing from overseas facilities and commercial supply. The new operating name is retained.
- FLYHT’s current site identifies FTG Aerospace Calgary. Its software roles are experienced roles, not entry-level opportunities.
- Carbonova has a documented pilot and a separate larger demonstration project. Funding or an active project label is not treated as commissioned commercial production.
- A GeologicAI posting visible in search returned Page Not Found in fresh original-page extraction. It is not proposed as a current card.
- Foremost’s fresh career extraction still showed Loading positions. Its facility extraction failed. The draft does not assign a particular vehicle model to Calgary or infer that the listed roles are accepting applications.

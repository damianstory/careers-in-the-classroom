# Made by Marcus

CGY-211-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

When would a seasonal shop be a better choice than a year-round location?

## Company and Calgary connection

Made by Marcus operates Calgary scoop shops. Its Inglewood location is described as a summer walk-up concept that hosts ice-cream workshops during fall and winter.

The company lists multiple Calgary shops, including the seasonal Inglewood location.

## How it works

1. Identify who uses the location and when their needs change.
2. Compare a seasonal service with a year-round alternative.
3. Estimate the resources and costs each option would require.
4. Test the most uncertain assumption before choosing how to use the space.

## Classroom activity

Compare two invented winter plans for an ice-cream shop: regular scoop service or small-group workshops. Give each a hypothetical attendance range, staffing cost and material cost. Calculate a simple contribution estimate, then name a cost or constraint your model left out.

**Discuss:** When might serving fewer customers create more value than keeping the same service open all year?

**Limits:** All demand and cost figures are classroom inventions, not Made by Marcus financial information. The source establishes the seasonal format, not its profitability or the reason management selected it. Workshop availability can change.

# OptiSeis Solutions

CGY-189-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How can you collect useful seismic information while disturbing less land?

## Company and Calgary connection

OptiSeis designs seismic surveys and advises on acquisition. Its services connect source and sensor placement, subsurface imaging requirements and environmental planning.

OptiSeis lists Calgary as its operating contact location. Survey field sites are separate from this Calgary design-work connection.

## How it works

1. Define the underground question and the surface constraints.
2. Choose possible positions for sources and receivers.
3. Compare predicted coverage with access and disturbance constraints.
4. Check collected data and document the trade-offs in the chosen survey.

## Classroom activity

Draw a fictional 6-by-6 landscape grid containing a wetland and an existing access route. Place up to eight paper receivers and two sources. Compare two layouts for coverage and access, then list what a real wave model would still need to tell you. Do not treat the number of grid squares as a measurement of seismic image quality.

**Discuss:** When could collecting fewer measurements make an interpretation less reliable, even if it reduces surface disturbance?

**Limits:** The grid is an invented planning exercise, not an OptiSeis geometry or a field-work instruction. Environmental improvement and image quality must be assessed for a specific survey; company claims are not a guarantee. No survey site is placed in Calgary without evidence.

## Course connection

Physics 20: Mechanical waves and sampling a reflected signal. Editorial application of the official course reference; not a claim that the company follows this curriculum.

## Related work and pathways

- Geophysicist: Plan source and receiver layouts, evaluate wave measurements and explain the limits of an underground interpretation.

- University of Calgary — Geophysics (BSc): Physics and quantitative methods for investigating the Earth. A related degree foundation. Professional registration and relevant experience are separate requirements; consult the current provider and regulator. This is not an employer hiring requirement.

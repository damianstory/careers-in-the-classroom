# BioAro

CGY-185-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How does a genomics laboratory check whether a result can be trusted?

## Company and Calgary connection

BioAro describes genomics testing and laboratory quality-control work. Its Calgary Lab Director description includes validating methods, checking equipment and reviewing the reproducibility of results.

BioAro identifies Calgary laboratory operations and a Calgary head office.

## How it works

1. Keep each sample connected to its identifier and handling record.
2. Run the method with appropriate controls and check that the equipment is working.
3. Compare repeated measurements and investigate disagreements before interpreting them.
4. Document the result, its limitations and any need for a repeat test.

## Classroom activity

Give groups three fictional sequence reads: ACGTAC, ACGTAC and ACGTTC. Ask them to locate the disagreement, propose an explanation and decide what evidence they would request next. Then reveal that a control failed: should the result still be reported? This paper exercise uses no biological samples or personal genetic information.

**Discuss:** When two measurements agree, what else would you need to know before trusting them?

**Limits:** The activity illustrates quality-control reasoning, not a clinical sequencing method or diagnosis. BioAro’s source describes an experienced director with doctoral-level qualifications; the diploma below is a related laboratory foundation, not qualification for that post or regulated clinical practice.

# Launchcode

CGY-201-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How can an app turn a messy scheduling problem into clear decisions?

## Company and Calgary connection

Launchcode’s portfolio includes SOL, a scheduling tool paired with an employee app. The studio describes discovery, design and software development as parts of its work.

Launchcode lists a Calgary office on its own website. This is the lc.dev studio, distinct from the US training nonprofit with a similar name.

## How it works

1. Identify the people, time slots and constraints a schedule must represent.
2. Express availability and conflicts as explicit rules.
3. Apply the rules to propose assignments.
4. Test difficult cases and explain why an assignment was accepted or rejected.

## Classroom activity

Create a fictional school-club roster with four volunteers and three sessions. Write pseudocode that checks availability and prevents overlapping assignments. Add a case where no volunteer is available and decide how the program should report it rather than inventing a valid assignment.

**Discuss:** What is the difference between finding a possible schedule and finding a fair one?

**Limits:** The activity is an invented rule-based exercise, not SOL’s AI model or a claim about its results. No employee records or private customer data are used.

## Course connection

CSE1120 · Structured Programming 2: selected outcomes 1, 1.9, 2, 3.1. Decisions, repetition and test cases. The company activity is an editorial application.

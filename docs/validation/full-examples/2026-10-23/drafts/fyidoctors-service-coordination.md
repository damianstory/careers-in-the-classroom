# FYidoctors — Calgary clinics

CGY-217-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How can a clinic organize a service around different patient needs?

## Company and Calgary connection

FYidoctors’ Calgary service page describes local eye-care clinics, appointment booking and insurance billing. These services connect clinical work with the practical experience of arranging a visit.

FYidoctors maintains a dedicated Calgary clinic page and location directory.

## How it works

1. Understand what administrative help a visitor needs.
2. Arrange an appropriate appointment and explain the next step.
3. Check required records while protecting personal information.
4. Coordinate follow-up and refer clinical questions to the qualified care team.

## Classroom activity

Map a fictional visitor journey from booking to arrival to follow-up. Include someone who needs accessibility information and someone unsure about a billing document. Mark which questions an administrative worker can answer and which must go to a clinician. Use no medical records or diagnoses.

**Discuss:** How can a service be easy to navigate without asking people for information it does not need?

**Limits:** This is a service-design exercise, not clinical advice or a description of FYidoctors’ internal workflow. Medical office preparation does not qualify someone as an optometrist or optician. The company’s lens laboratory is not described as Calgary-based.

## Related work and pathways

- Medical office and booking support: Coordinate booking and administrative information while directing clinical questions to qualified practitioners.

- SAIT — Medical Office Assistant and Unit Clerk: Clinical administration and patient-record workflows. An administrative route, distinct from sonography and physician training. Check current admissions and intake before applying; this draft does not promise entry or employment.

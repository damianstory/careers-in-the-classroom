# EPT Clean Oil

CGY-188-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

What can an oil sample tell us about a machine before it fails?

## Company and Calgary connection

EPT Clean Oil offers lubricant analysis through its Fluid Technical Center. Chemists interpret sample results and use laboratory-scale treatment equipment to investigate oil-condition problems.

EPT lists its head office in Calgary. Its company history documents expanding Calgary laboratory space; the current services page describes the Fluid Technical Center.

## How it works

1. Record where and when the oil sample was collected.
2. Measure relevant properties instead of judging condition from colour alone.
3. Compare a sample before and after a controlled laboratory treatment.
4. Interpret the results with the equipment context and recommend further investigation.

## Classroom activity

Use fictional oil reports for three samples with different water, particle and acidity trends. Choose which follow-up question each report raises, then compare a before-and-after treatment pair. Explain why removing visible particles would not prove that every dissolved contaminant was removed.

**Discuss:** How could an oil sample look cleaner while still needing further investigation?

**Limits:** Use invented reports only, with no machine-operating thresholds or maintenance instructions. Laboratory treatment is evidence for investigation, not a guarantee of on-site performance. The old sample-submission download is not used as a current source.

# Absolute Imaging

CGY-190-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How do you recover a useful underground signal from noisy measurements?

## Company and Calgary connection

Absolute Imaging processes seismic data using software that includes noise suppression and imaging tools. Its technology page identifies a Calgary processing centre and an in-house research and development team.

The company explicitly lists a Calgary seismic-data processing centre.

## How it works

1. Preserve the original wave measurements and acquisition information.
2. Identify unwanted signals and choose a suitable processing approach.
3. Compare processed traces with the originals to check what changed.
4. Build an interpretation while reporting uncertainty and possible processing artefacts.

## Classroom activity

Give students three fictional traces with the same pulse but different added noise. Align the traces and calculate the mean at each time step. Compare the result with a trace where the pulse arrives later. Ask what goes wrong if measurements are averaged without alignment.

**Discuss:** How could making a graph look smoother also remove information that matters?

**Limits:** Averaging is a simplified illustration of signal and noise, not a reproduction of Absolute Imaging’s proprietary algorithms. An underground image is an interpretation of measurements, not a photograph or proof of a resource.

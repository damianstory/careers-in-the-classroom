# TELUS Spark Science Centre

CGY-198-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How can one robot arm create different experiences of motion?

## Company and Calgary connection

TELUS Spark’s RO5iE robot ride offers different motion experiences. Spark also describes a past robotics workshop led by BEC Robotics, the supplier identified as creating the ride.

TELUS Spark operates the science centre and RO5iE experience in Calgary.

## How it works

1. Define the movement a robot is intended to make and its physical limits.
2. Break the task into an ordered sequence of actions.
3. Use sensor information to check position or nearby obstacles.
4. Test in a simulation and revise the sequence when the observed motion differs.

## Classroom activity

Use a simulated tabletop robot arm or a paper model. Plan a path through three positions, then introduce an obstacle and a restricted joint range. Revise the plan and explain why the same destination can be reached through different movements.

**Discuss:** What information does a robot need before a sequence of commands becomes a safe, repeatable movement?

**Limits:** Spark operates and interprets the experience; BEC Robotics is credited with making RO5iE. The workshop page describes a past event, not a current booking. The classroom simulation is not ride-control code or a procedure for moving people.

## Course connection

CSE1240 · Robotics Programming 1: selected outcomes 1.3, 1.4, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3. Robot tasks, environments, movement and capabilities. The company activity is an editorial application.

## Related work and pathways

- Science educator: Help learners connect a robot’s programmed movements and sensor inputs to an understandable explanation.

- University of Calgary — Physics (BSc): Physics, mathematical modelling, experimental observation and problem-solving. A subject-knowledge foundation for explaining motion and robotics. It is not teacher certification or an employer requirement; science communication and teaching practice need additional preparation.

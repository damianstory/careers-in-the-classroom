# Calgary Farmers' Market

CGY-213-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How can a market choose a mix of vendors that gives people a reason to return?

## Company and Calgary connection

Calgary Farmers’ Market operates South and West venues. Its vendor page prioritizes businesses that make, bake or grow their products and considers fit with the market’s vision and available space.

The market identifies its South venue on 77th Avenue SE and West venue on Greenbriar Drive NW in Calgary.

## How it works

1. Define the experience the market wants to offer visitors.
2. Review a vendor’s products and how they fit the market’s purpose.
3. Check whether an appropriate space is available.
4. Compare the overall mix and identify gaps or unnecessary duplication.

## Classroom activity

Give groups six invented vendor applications and three spaces with different size and utility constraints. Choose a mix using a stated market purpose and customer needs. Explain why the highest expected sales alone may not determine the best mix.

**Discuss:** How can two individually attractive vendors become a poor combination for the same market?

**Limits:** Applications and selection scores are fictional. The operator and individual vendors are distinct organizations and employers. A published application form does not mean a space is available or an applicant will be accepted.

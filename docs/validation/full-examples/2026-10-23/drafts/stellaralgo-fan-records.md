# StellarAlgo

CGY-208-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How can a team recognize the same fan across different ticket records?

## Company and Calgary connection

A Ticketmaster case study describes StellarAlgo working with the Calgary Flames during the 2021–22 season to organize ticketing data and support audience analysis.

The Calgary Flames partnership is documented in Ticketmaster’s November 2022 case study; it is a historical Calgary project.

## How it works

1. Identify what each ticket and audience record represents.
2. Use suitable identifiers to connect records without assuming a name is unique.
3. Flag uncertain matches and preserve the source of each record.
4. Summarize the linked records only for a defined, appropriate question.

## Classroom activity

Create fictional Fans, Games and Tickets tables using invented IDs. Include two people with the same name and a ticket bought for a guest. Compare matching by name with matching by ID, then explain why a purchaser is not necessarily every person who attended.

**Discuss:** What can a ticket purchase tell you about attendance, and what does it leave unknown?

**Limits:** The case is from 2021–22, not a current-results claim. All classroom records are synthetic. The exercise does not reproduce StellarAlgo’s proprietary matching method, establish consent for marketing or treat ticket purchasers as confirmed attendees.

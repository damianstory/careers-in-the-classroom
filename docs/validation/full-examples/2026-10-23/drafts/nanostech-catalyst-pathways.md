# NanosTech

CGY-186-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How can a catalyst change the route from a raw material to a useful product?

## Company and Calgary connection

NanosTech develops catalysts in Calgary. Its AQP Bio process is designed to upgrade biomass-derived oils into material suitable for further fuel processing.

The company describes its catalyst platform as built in Calgary.

## How it works

1. Identify the properties of the incoming biomass-derived oil.
2. Bring the feedstock into a controlled catalytic process.
3. Examine the resulting material to see how its properties have changed.
4. Compare results under stated conditions before deciding whether further development is justified.

## Classroom activity

Build a paper process map from wood residues to biomass-derived oil, then to upgraded oil and further fuel processing. Mark where heat, other inputs and measurements would be needed. Compare two fictional trials with different product compositions; explain why a change in colour alone would not establish successful upgrading.

**Discuss:** What would you measure to distinguish a useful chemical conversion from a change in appearance?

**Limits:** The diagram is a classroom process map, not NanosTech’s reactor design. Company statements about selectivity, emissions, cost or commercial performance are not independent measurements. A catalyst does not supply all the energy a process needs or make atoms disappear.

## Course connection

Chemistry 30: Organic feedstocks, chemical conversion and catalyst-assisted processes. Editorial application of the official course reference; not a claim that the company follows this curriculum.

## Related work and pathways

- Process engineering: Compare feedstocks, reaction conditions and product requirements when designing a conversion process.
- Laboratory analysis: Measure changes in samples to evaluate a proposed catalyst and process conditions.

- University of Calgary — Chemical Engineering (BSc): Engineering foundations, process design and a final-year team design project, with an internship opportunity. An undergraduate engineering route. The Stantec job example asks for a relevant degree and EIT registration or eligibility; E3’s role additionally asks for substantial experience.
- SAIT — Chemical Laboratory Technology: Chemical analysis, samples and laboratory practice. A laboratory route; not a medical licence or automatic research-scientist qualification. Check current admissions and intake before applying; this draft does not promise entry or employment.

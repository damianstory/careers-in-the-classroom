# Allnorth — Calgary operations

CGY-194-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

How does a sensor reading become a control decision in an industrial system?

## Company and Calgary connection

Allnorth provides instrumentation and control-system engineering, including control strategies, system specifications and integration. Its expanded capabilities include the acquired Dynamysk automation team.

Allnorth lists Calgary NW and Calgary SE offices. The services described span the company; a particular plant or project is not assigned to Calgary.

## How it works

1. Measure a variable and check the sensor’s range and units.
2. Compare the reading with the intended operating condition.
3. Use a defined rule to decide whether the output should change.
4. Measure again and investigate unexpected behaviour or missing signals.

## Classroom activity

Model a fictional room controller on paper. Turn a fan on above 26 degrees and off below 24 degrees; keep its previous state in between. Test readings of 23, 25, 27, 25 and 23, then a missing reading. Explain why the two thresholds reduce repeated switching.

**Discuss:** What should a controller do when the sensor stops supplying trustworthy information?

**Limits:** This classroom feedback model is not an Allnorth procedure, safety design or plant-control specification. Its thresholds are invented. The Science 10 connection is a general measurement application; no exact provincial unit outcome is asserted.

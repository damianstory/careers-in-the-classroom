# PBA Group

CGY-218-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

When might an empty office building become a useful hotel?

## Company and Calgary connection

PBA Group describes converting the former Canadian Centre office building into Element Calgary Downtown. The project adapts existing space for hospitality, including longer stays.

The documented conversion is at 888 4th Avenue SW in downtown Calgary.

## How it works

1. Identify a customer need that an existing building might serve.
2. Compare the proposed use with the building’s constraints.
3. Estimate conversion costs, operating needs and demand under different assumptions.
4. Decide what evidence is still needed before treating the proposal as viable.

## Classroom activity

Compare fictional office and extended-stay hotel uses for one building. Use invented room demand, conversion cost and operating-cost ranges, then change one assumption. Explain why potential revenue alone cannot decide whether the conversion is worthwhile.

**Discuss:** Which assumption would you investigate first if getting it wrong could make the whole project unworkable?

**Limits:** The classroom numbers are invented and do not represent PBA’s financing or profitability. Business-advisory work is an interpreted connection, not proof of an employee title. Building suitability requires specialist assessment beyond this exercise.

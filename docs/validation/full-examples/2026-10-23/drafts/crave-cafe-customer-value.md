# Crave Cupcakes

CGY-215-FULL v1 · 2026-10-23

## Classroom question

When does adding a cafe strengthen a bakery's customer value?

## Company and Calgary connection

Crave distinguishes its classic bakeries from café-format locations. Its Hillhurst Calgary location includes CeCe’s Coffee alongside the bakery offering.

Crave lists Hillhurst and several other Calgary bakery locations on its official location page.

## How it works

1. Identify the customer need served by the existing bakery.
2. Describe what coffee service and seating could add for a different visit.
3. Estimate the extra resources and coordination the format requires.
4. Compare customer evidence and operating constraints before judging the change.

## Classroom activity

Create two fictional customer profiles: someone collecting a birthday order and someone seeking a short morning meeting spot. Design a bakery-only and a café-plus-bakery offer. List the extra equipment, labour and space, then propose one small test of demand.

**Discuss:** When can adding more services make an offer less useful for its original customers?

**Limits:** The activity does not report Crave’s revenue, margins or actual decision process. Coffee service and baking involve different tasks; a café format is not evidence that every employee performs both.

## Course connection

ENT2010 · Analyzing Ventures: selected outcomes 1.1.2, 1.1.5, 1.1.7, 2.1.3, 2.1.10, 2.1.11. Customer needs, product quality and alternative venture formats. The company activity is an editorial application.

## Related work and pathways

- Retail operations coordinator: Compare how a café format changes the customer experience, staffing and daily operations.
- Commercial baking: Plan consistent baked products and coordinate production with the service format.

- SAIT — Business Administration — Management diploma: Business operations and organizational decisions. Broad business preparation; management and coordination roles also depend on relevant experience.
- SAIT — Baking and Pastry Arts: Practise bakery and pastry production and product quality. Check current admission, portfolio, intake and workplace requirements; program completion does not guarantee a job or professional registration.

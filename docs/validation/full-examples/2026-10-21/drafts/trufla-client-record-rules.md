## How can a self-service form give someone the right information without mixing up their records?

Trufla develops technology for insurance brokers and their customers. Its truMobile service supports self-service access and connections to client records.

**Calgary connection:** Trufla describes Calgary and Cairo operations. Its careers page identifies developers, designers, product owners and business analysts without assigning every role to Calgary.

## How it works

1. Identify the request and the account it belongs to.
2. Check that the user is allowed to access the requested information.
3. Validate the submitted fields before using them in the workflow.
4. Return a clear result or correction request and preserve the record of the interaction.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Software and data

Software developers translate user needs into data structures, rules and interfaces. They test how the system behaves, investigate errors and improve the way people find and use information.

**At this organization:** Implement decision rules and test ordinary, missing and unauthorized inputs.

- **Understand the task:** Clarify users, records and the decisions the software needs to support.
- **Build and connect:** Implement data relationships, processing rules and useful interfaces.
- **Test and improve:** Check expected results, missing inputs and access rules, then investigate failures.

**Collaborators:** Product designers, Users, Technical specialists, Operations teams

**Classroom connection:** Information helps only when people can find it, trust it and use it for the intended decision.

## Why is selling an appliance only one part of delivering value to a customer?

Trail Appliances' Alberta operation sells appliances and coordinates services around the purchase. Its Calgary South location offers a local example of connecting product choice, availability and fulfilment.

**Calgary connection:** The original Alberta store page identifies the South Calgary showroom at 6880–11 Street SE. Alberta and BC operations are kept distinct.

## How it works

1. Understand the customer's space, needs and constraints.
2. Compare suitable products and confirm availability.
3. Coordinate the agreed pickup or delivery arrangements.
4. Check that the handoff meets the customer's needs and address remaining questions.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Retail operations coordinator

Connect day-to-day service with the resources a retail business needs.

**At this organization:** Clarify customer needs and coordinate product information and service expectations.

- **Observe:** Identify customer needs and service constraints.
- **Coordinate:** Organize tasks and resources to deliver the service.
- **Review:** Compare outcomes and trade-offs before changing a process.

**Collaborators:** Store teams, Customer-service teams, Finance colleagues

**Classroom connection:** Customer benefit and operating costs need to be considered together.

### Warehouse and inventory operations

Receive, locate and move goods while keeping identity and handling requirements visible.

**At this organization:** Keep product identity and availability connected to the pickup or delivery handoff.

- **Check:** Check incoming goods against shipment information.
- **Move:** Move and store products using appropriate equipment and procedures.
- **Maintain:** Maintain inventory records and coordinate outbound loads.

**Collaborators:** Drivers, Dispatch, Food-quality teams

**Classroom connection:** Temperature, time and correct identification all matter to a cold chain.

**Potential preparation:** [SAIT — Business Administration — Supply Chain Management](https://www.sait.ca/programs-and-courses/diplomas/business-administration-supply-chain-management) (Diploma; Calgary). Supply-chain coordination, operations and planning.

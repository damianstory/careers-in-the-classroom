CGY-171-FULL · version 1 · final-copy decision: **pending**

## What evidence would help decide whether a business idea can work?

ATB Financial supports business customers and entrepreneurs with banking and planning resources. Its tools help people examine customers, startup costs and business assumptions.

**Calgary connection:** ATB identifies a Calgary Entrepreneur Centre associated with its Uptown 17th Avenue branch.

## How it works

1. Describe the customer problem and the proposed benefit.
2. Gather evidence about customers, alternatives and the resources needed.
3. Compare a simple revenue-and-cost projection with the assumptions behind it.
4. Revise the idea or identify the next question to test.

## Classroom connection

**Analyzing Ventures** — selected scope: 1.1.2, 1.1.5, 1.1.7, 2.1.3

**Try it:** Evaluate an invented bicycle-cleaning service. Estimate demand and startup costs, then compare a low-demand and high-demand scenario using the same stated assumptions.

**Discuss:** Which assumption would you investigate first before committing money to the idea?

**Model limits:** This is a classroom venture exercise, not lending advice or a promise of ATB financing. A planning template is not evidence that a specific business will succeed.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Business adviser

Help people examine a business idea, its evidence and the assumptions behind a plan.

**At this organization:** Help a business owner clarify assumptions, compare evidence and plan the next useful investigation.

- **Clarify:** Identify the customer need and the decision the owner is trying to make.
- **Compare:** Examine costs, demand assumptions and possible alternatives.
- **Plan:** Identify a useful next test or source of specialist advice.

**Collaborators:** Business owners, Accounting specialists, Banking teams

**Classroom connection:** A business proposal can be improved by testing its assumptions before committing resources.

**Potential preparation:** [SAIT — Business Administration — Management diploma](https://www.sait.ca/programs-and-courses/diplomas/business-administration-management) (Diploma; Calgary). Business operations and organizational decisions.

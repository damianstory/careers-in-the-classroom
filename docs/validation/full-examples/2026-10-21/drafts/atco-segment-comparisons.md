## How can one company's total results hide differences between its businesses?

ATCO operates across several businesses and reports results by segment. Its corporate materials help illustrate why a total can conceal different underlying trends.

**Calgary connection:** ATCO's annual report identifies its Calgary corporate address. Group operations and subsidiary work take place in multiple locations.

## How it works

1. Define the businesses included in each segment and the period being compared.
2. Use consistent measures before comparing the segment results.
3. Separate changes in operating performance from changes in reporting structure.
4. Explain how the segment patterns contribute to the group total.

**Discuss:** When can a year-to-year comparison become misleading even if every number is correct?

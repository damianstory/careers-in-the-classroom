## How would you tell whether replacing lawn with native plants is helping an ecosystem?

Earthmaster is an environmental consulting company. Its Calgary roadside project replaced maintained turf with native grasses and wildflowers.

**Calgary connection:** The City identifies the 16 Avenue roadside pilot as beginning in 2020. Earthmaster describes its work on a five-hectare Calgary site.

## How it works

1. Choose a restoration goal and record the site's starting condition.
2. Prepare the site and establish a mix of grasses and wildflowers suited to it.
3. Repeat observations of plant cover and habitat conditions using a consistent method.
4. Compare the evidence over time before deciding whether the intervention is meeting its goal.

## Classroom connection

**Biology 20** — selected scope: 20–B1.3k, 20–B1.1sts

**Try it:** Use invented observations from three equal-sized plots before and after restoration. Compare native plant cover and the number of plant types, then identify one uncontrolled difference between the plots.

**Discuss:** Would more flowers alone establish that the whole ecosystem is healthier?

**Model limits:** The sampling exercise is a teaching model. The source describes establishment and intended benefits; it does not supply a controlled test proving pollinator recovery or emissions reductions.

Collect and interpret evidence about a place before recommending an action.

**At this organization:** Record vegetation and site conditions consistently so changes can be compared.

### Conservation research and population ecology

Conservation researchers choose ecological questions, plan comparisons and interpret evidence about living populations and their habitats. Measures can include survival, reproduction, abundance or vegetation; the choice depends on the question.

**At this organization:** Frame a restoration question and interpret ecological evidence without treating correlation as proof.

- **Plan a comparison:** Choose relevant ecological measures and a defensible comparison.
- **Observe consistently:** Record organisms and environmental conditions using repeatable methods.
- **Interpret the evidence:** Consider detection limits and alternative explanations before drawing conclusions.

**Collaborators:** Field technicians, Land managers, Other scientists

**Classroom connection:** A visible change does not by itself establish the cause of ecological recovery.

**Potential preparation:** [University of Calgary — Biological Sciences BSc](https://www.ucalgary.ca/future-students/undergraduate/explore-programs/biological-sciences) (Degree; Calgary). Biological systems, research and laboratory investigation.

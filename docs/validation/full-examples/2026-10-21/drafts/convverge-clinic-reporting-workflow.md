## How can a reporting portal collect the right records from the right people?

Convverge develops business applications and digital workflows. Its South Calgary Primary Care Network case study describes replacing a difficult-to-maintain reporting portal with Microsoft Power Pages.

**Calgary connection:** The named client serves clinics in South Calgary. Convverge also lists Calgary among its operating locations.

## How it works

1. Understand who submits reports and what information the organization needs.
2. Prototype the workflow and review it with the people who will use it.
3. Connect access and submission steps so users can provide the appropriate records.
4. Test ordinary and exceptional cases, then maintain the workflow as requirements change.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Software and data

Software developers translate user needs into data structures, rules and interfaces. They test how the system behaves, investigate errors and improve the way people find and use information.

**At this organization:** Translate a reporting need into forms, decisions and testable workflow behaviour.

- **Understand the task:** Clarify users, records and the decisions the software needs to support.
- **Build and connect:** Implement data relationships, processing rules and useful interfaces.
- **Test and improve:** Check expected results, missing inputs and access rules, then investigate failures.

**Collaborators:** Product designers, Users, Technical specialists, Operations teams

**Classroom connection:** Information helps only when people can find it, trust it and use it for the intended decision.

**Potential preparation:** [SAIT — Software Development](https://www.sait.ca/programs-and-courses/diplomas/software-development) (Diploma; Calgary). Learn to design, test and build software across front-end and back-end systems.

### Technical application support

Help people use a technical product in the conditions of their own workplace.

**At this organization:** Help users identify a submission problem and communicate it so the system can be improved.

- **Ask:** Ask what the user is trying to measure or control.
- **Separate:** Separate a setup problem from a sensor or process problem.
- **Explain:** Explain the next diagnostic step and record what changed.

**Collaborators:** Customers, Product developers, Field technicians

**Classroom connection:** Troubleshooting means testing explanations one at a time.

**Potential preparation:** [SAIT — Software Development](https://www.sait.ca/programs-and-courses/diplomas/software-development) (Diploma; Calgary). Learn to design, test and build software across front-end and back-end systems.

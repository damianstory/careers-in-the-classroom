## How can a concert team know which shirt sizes are left at each stand?

atVenu builds commerce software for live events. Its merchandise tools track inventory by stand, item and size so teams can see what is available across an event.

**Calgary connection:** The employer's own career page identifies Calgary as its technology and product hub, distinct from its California head office and UK locations.

## How it works

1. Identify each item and size and record its starting quantity at each stand.
2. Record sales and transfers against the correct item and location.
3. Update the remaining quantities and flag a stand running low.
4. Compare the records with physical counts and investigate differences.

## Why must a measurement stay connected to its equipment, time and location?

Validere provides software and services for industrial operations and environmental information. Its platform connects field observations and operational records to reporting workflows.

**Calgary connection:** Validere's current original contact page lists Calgary, Houston and Toronto offices. It does not establish that every product function is carried out by the Calgary team.

## How it works

1. Connect an observation to the equipment, location and time it describes.
2. Preserve the units, method and source needed to interpret that observation.
3. Check related records for gaps or inconsistent values before combining them.
4. Keep an audit trail so a report can be traced back to its supporting information.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Software and data

Software developers translate user needs into data structures, rules and interfaces. They test how the system behaves, investigate errors and improve the way people find and use information.

**At this organization:** Maintain relationships, validation checks and traceable changes across operational records.

- **Understand the task:** Clarify users, records and the decisions the software needs to support.
- **Build and connect:** Implement data relationships, processing rules and useful interfaces.
- **Test and improve:** Check expected results, missing inputs and access rules, then investigate failures.

**Collaborators:** Product designers, Users, Technical specialists, Operations teams

**Classroom connection:** Information helps only when people can find it, trust it and use it for the intended decision.

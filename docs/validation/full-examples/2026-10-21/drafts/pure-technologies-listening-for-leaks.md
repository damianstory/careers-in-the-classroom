## How could listening inside a water pipe help locate a leak without digging it up?

Pure Technologies, a Xylem brand, provides pipeline inspection technology. A published Calgary case study describes acoustic inspection of operating water mains using SmartBall.

**Calgary connection:** The case study identifies three Calgary inspections in 2018, including the 52nd Street Southeast Feedermain. This is historical project evidence.

## How it works

1. Introduce the inspection tool through a suitable access point in the water main.
2. Let it travel with the water while its acoustic sensor records signals.
3. Track its movement so analysts can connect a signal with a position along the pipe.
4. Use the inspection findings to guide follow-up investigation and maintenance decisions.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Pipeline inspection technician

Collect and interpret inspection evidence to help locate parts of a pipeline that need follow-up.

**At this organization:** Deploy and track an inspection tool, review acoustic records and document locations needing follow-up.

- **Prepare:** Check the inspection plan, equipment and location records.
- **Inspect:** Track the tool and preserve the observations collected along its route.
- **Report:** Review unusual signals and document their location and uncertainty.

**Collaborators:** Water-system operators, Engineers, Data analysts

**Classroom connection:** A signal becomes useful when its origin, location and limitations are understood.

## How can a closed landfill keep affecting the water around it?

BGC Engineering provides applied earth-science and engineering services. Its Nose Creek project examines how a former landfill interacts with its surrounding environment.

**Calgary connection:** BGC identifies the City of Calgary as its client for the Nose Creek Inactive Landfill beside Nose Creek and the Bow River. The landfill operated until 1967.

## How it works

1. Build a conceptual picture of the old landfill, surrounding land uses and possible pathways.
2. Collect site observations and monitoring evidence to test that picture.
3. Consider where water, gas or direct contact could connect a source to a receptor.
4. Use the evidence to assess management options and decide what needs continued monitoring.

## Classroom connection

**Biology 20** — selected scope: 20–B1.3k, 20–B1.1sts

**Try it:** Draw a fictional site with soil layers, groundwater and a stream. Mark a possible source, pathway and receptor; identify a measurement that could support or contradict the pathway.

**Discuss:** What is the difference between a possible pathway and evidence that harm is occurring?

**Model limits:** BGC's undated project description discusses investigation and management measures. It is not a current public-health assessment. The classroom map is invented and does not locate contamination at the real site.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Environmental field assessment

Collect and interpret evidence about a place before recommending an action.

**At this organization:** Document site conditions and collect monitoring information that can be traced to a place and time.

- **Investigate:** Plan observations or samples around a clear question.
- **Test and compare:** Record locations, methods and conditions consistently.
- **Document and improve:** Explain the limits of the evidence and the next useful check.

**Collaborators:** Laboratory staff, engineers and project planners

**Classroom connection:** A result has meaning only in the context of how and where it was collected.

**Potential preparation:** [SAIT — Environmental Technology](https://www.sait.ca/programs-and-courses/diplomas/environmental-technology) (Diploma; Calgary). Study environmental monitoring, assessment and field methods.

### Geoscience and interpretation

Explain what rock measurements suggest about geology. Geoscientists combine observations with context and use additional tests where a scan leaves uncertainty.

**At this organization:** Interpret how subsurface materials and groundwater conditions affect possible transport pathways.

- **Investigate:** Describe core and identify changes along it.
- **Test and compare:** Compare sensor patterns with geological observations or assays.
- **Explain and improve:** Communicate alternative interpretations and uncertainty.

**Collaborators:** Core technicians, data scientists and exploration teams

**Classroom connection:** A measurement of a sample is not automatically a measurement of the whole deposit.

**Potential preparation:** [University of Calgary — Geoscience (BSc)](https://www.ucalgary.ca/future-students/undergraduate/explore-programs/geoscience) (Bachelor’s degree; Calgary). Study Earth materials through the geology program and its available study streams.

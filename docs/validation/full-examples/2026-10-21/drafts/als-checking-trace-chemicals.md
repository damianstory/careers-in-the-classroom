## How can a laboratory distinguish a trace chemical from a misleading signal?

ALS provides analytical laboratory services. Its May 2025 technical bulletin describes Calgary testing for alkanolamines and sulfolane in water using liquid chromatography and tandem mass spectrometry.

**Calgary connection:** The original technical bulletin specifically identifies the Calgary laboratory as offering this method.

## How it works

1. Preserve the identity of the water sample and select the analytical method.
2. Separate components with liquid chromatography before measuring selected ion signals.
3. Compare the signals with standards and use internal standards to help account for sample effects.
4. Report the concentration with the method's limitations; a signal needs interpretation.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Laboratory analysis

Make measurements that other people can trust. A laboratory worker prepares samples, uses a method consistently and records enough detail for someone else to interpret the result.

**At this organization:** Track sample identity, prepare standards and check analytical results before reporting.

- **Investigate:** Prepare and label samples so their identity is preserved.
- **Test and compare:** Run comparisons or reference samples to check the method.
- **Explain and improve:** Record results, units and limitations before sharing findings.

**Collaborators:** Scientists, process engineers and quality teams

**Classroom connection:** A repeatable method and a fair comparison matter as much as a single impressive number.

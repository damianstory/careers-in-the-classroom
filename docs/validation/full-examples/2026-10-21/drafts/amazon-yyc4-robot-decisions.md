## How does a warehouse robot decide where to move a container next?

Amazon's Calgary YYC4 fulfilment centre combines staff work with automated systems for handling customer orders. A May 2024 facility feature describes tote-sorting and transport robots.

**Calgary connection:** The original Amazon feature specifically identifies YYC4 in Calgary; it is not a description of the separate regional facilities.

## How it works

1. Identify the container and the destination required by the task.
2. Use the system's available inputs to decide the next action.
3. Move or sort the container within the permitted operating environment.
4. Check completion or a blocked condition before continuing.

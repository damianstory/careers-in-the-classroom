## How can measurements turn a real landscape into a useful 3D map?

McElhanney combines surveying, engineering and environmental work. Its geomatics teams turn spatial measurements into maps and models used in planning and construction.

**Calgary connection:** The Calgary branch lists surveyors, engineers, environmental specialists and technical staff. Company project evidence includes surveying for the Southwest Calgary Ring Road.

## How it works

1. Decide which features and dimensions the project needs to locate.
2. Collect spatial observations using an appropriate survey method.
3. Align and check observations before using them to create a map or surface model.
4. Compare the model with the design question and identify missing or uncertain detail.

## Classroom connection

**Science 10** — selected scope: Performing and Recording — measurement; Analyzing and Interpreting — uncertainty

**Try it:** Measure a small classroom area twice using a consistent reference point. Plot the coordinates, compare differences and discuss what a finer grid would reveal or miss.

**Discuss:** When does a more detailed map become more useful, and when might it still be misleading?

**Model limits:** The branch uses drones and 3D scanning, but the source does not identify every sensor used on the named road project. The activity teaches measurement and uncertainty, not a professional land survey or a proprietary LiDAR algorithm.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Spatial data and mapping

Turn measurements tied to places into usable spatial information. The work involves coordinates, scale and checking that datasets describe the same location and time.

**At this organization:** Check coordinates, organize spatial observations and prepare a usable map or model.

- **Investigate:** Check coordinate systems and measurement dates.
- **Test and compare:** Align and inspect spatial datasets.
- **Explain and improve:** Present maps or models with their limitations.

**Collaborators:** Surveyors, engineers, field operators and software teams

**Classroom connection:** Models can be detailed while still containing gaps or misaligned measurements.

**Potential preparation:** [SAIT — Geomatics Engineering Technology](https://www.sait.ca/programs-and-courses/diplomas/geomatics-engineering-technology) (Diploma; Calgary). Learn surveying, GIS, positioning and remote sensing.

### Drone operations

Plan and carry out flights for a specific task while coordinating with the people responsible for the site. The aircraft is one tool within an operational plan.

**At this organization:** Plan aerial data collection and check whether the observations suit the mapping task.

- **Investigate:** Review the task, site and operating constraints.
- **Test and compare:** Check the aircraft and coordinate the flight with relevant personnel.
- **Explain and improve:** Record observations and equipment issues after the flight.

**Collaborators:** Site operations, wildlife specialists and data analysts

**Classroom connection:** Flight performance must be considered alongside the task and the surrounding environment.

**Potential preparation:** [SAIT — Geomatics Engineering Technology](https://www.sait.ca/programs-and-courses/diplomas/geomatics-engineering-technology) (Diploma; Calgary). Learn surveying, GIS, positioning and remote sensing.

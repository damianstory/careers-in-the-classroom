## How can a field record become an invoice without being entered twice?

Aimsio builds software connecting field operations and office records. Its field-ticket feature records labour, equipment and materials and routes submitted tickets through approval before invoicing.

**Calgary connection:** Aimsio lists Calgary among its company locations. Its careers page describes a remote team across Canada and the United States; this is not an on-site-only Calgary workforce.

## How it works

1. Record the job, quantities and relevant work details in a field ticket.
2. Attach supporting information and send the ticket for review.
3. Resolve questions and record approval before converting the ticket to an invoice.
4. Carry the approved line items and rates into billing without entering them again.

## Roles and learning pathways

### Software and data

Software developers translate user needs into data structures, rules and interfaces. They test how the system behaves, investigate errors and improve the way people find and use information.

**At this organization:** Connect related records, implement checks and test how changes move through a workflow.

- **Understand the task:** Clarify users, records and the decisions the software needs to support.
- **Build and connect:** Implement data relationships, processing rules and useful interfaces.
- **Test and improve:** Check expected results, missing inputs and access rules, then investigate failures.

**Collaborators:** Product designers, Users, Technical specialists, Operations teams

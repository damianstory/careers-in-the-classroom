## How it works

1. A transducer sends a pulse of high-frequency sound into the body.
2. Some sound is reflected at boundaries where acoustic properties change and returns toward the transducer.
3. The machine uses returned signals to help construct an image. Travel time can contribute to depth estimation; a simple model assumes a sound speed.
4. A sonographer acquires useful views while caring for the patient. A clinician interprets imaging evidence with the clinical question; an image alone is not the whole decision.

The explanations above are a classroom model grounded in the company/work sources and curriculum source, not a proprietary process specification.

## Classroom conversation

Why is finding an echo different from knowing what it means?

**Activity:** Use a fictional uniform-medium model with speed 1,500 m/s and an echo returning after 80 microseconds. Calculate the total sound path (0.12 m) and boundary depth (0.06 m). Explain the division by two and one reason a real examination is more complicated.

**Teacher note:** The assumed speed and timing are teaching values, not a patient result. Ultrasound is a mechanical sound wave, not X-rays. Do not diagnose from images or imply that sound travels at one exact speed through every tissue.

### Diagnostic medical sonographer

Acquire ultrasound images while supporting the person being examined.

- Prepare for the requested examination and communicate with the patient.
- Position the transducer and adjust acquisition settings to obtain useful views.
- Document the images for interpretation within the clinical team's workflow.

**Works with:** Patients, Radiologists, Clinic staff.

**Classroom connection:** Producing a clear measurement and interpreting its meaning are connected but different tasks.

**Related routes in this draft:** SAIT — Diagnostic Medical Sonography. These are connections to explore, not employer hiring requirements.

### Medical office and booking support

Help patients and clinical teams coordinate the information and appointments needed for care.

- Handle appointment requests and confirm required information.
- Communicate clearly with patients and modality teams.
- Keep registration and scheduling records accurate.

**Works with:** Patients, Technologists, Booking teams.

**Classroom connection:** Care depends on reliable human coordination as well as instruments.

**Related routes in this draft:** SAIT — Medical Office Assistant and Unit Clerk. These are connections to explore, not employer hiring requirements.

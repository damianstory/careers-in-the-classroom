## What the deeper research changed

- **Standen’s:** confirmed specific modelling/manufacturing methods and its 2026 distribution partnership. The story stays about manufacturing; numerical spring data are explicitly invented.
- **EFW:** employer descriptions now support sonography duties and credentials, plus a separate medical-office route. Those evergreen descriptions are not treated as vacancies.
- **Alberta Boot:** replaced the generic mechanical-design route with a documented route from high-school retail work into production responsibility, alongside clearly labelled adjacent arts/material study. No invented apprenticeship.
- **MOBILTEX:** strengthened the explicit curriculum connection to corrosion and cathodic protection; clarified that monitoring and protection are different functions.
- **ICE Western:** selected a concrete run-around heat-recovery mechanism from an employer-hosted historical catalogue. The copy preserves its age and does not imply current product availability.
- **NanoTess:** added a company source specifically identifying Calgary development/manufacture; omitted clinical efficacy and regulatory claims that this pass did not independently establish.
- **BURNCO and OPI:** preserved the verified Calgary operating connection without inventing a specific local laboratory, customer site or manufacturing assignment.

## Decisions made in drafting

- BURNCO uses Science 10 chemical change, not a claim that a heterogeneous concrete mixture is a solution.
- Alberta Boot uses a clearly separated friction investigation; the company sources do not establish a particular sole-testing method.
- NanoTess uses general catalysis, with no claim that the classroom model establishes clinical effectiveness.
- Global Power Technologies, ENMAX, ICE Western and Congebec share Science 10 energy concepts but use different systems: direct thermoelectric conversion, combined-cycle generation, heat recovery and cold storage.
- OPI uses Biology 20 respiration; a CO2 reading remains indirect evidence with alternative explanations.
- Hifi distinguishes electromagnetic light in a fibre from mechanical disturbances.
- MOBILTEX has an explicit Chemistry 30 cathodic-protection connection; the monitor remains separate from the protection system.

Exact industrial processes, safety thresholds and clinical decisions are not taught as required curriculum outcomes.

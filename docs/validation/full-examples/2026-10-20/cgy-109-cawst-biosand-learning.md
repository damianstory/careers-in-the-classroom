## Question

How can layers of sand and living organisms help treat water?

A water-treatment device depends on materials, living processes and people knowing how to use it.

## About the organization

CAWST provides water, sanitation and hygiene education and technical support.

Who it serves: Organizations and practitioners improving water and sanitation services.

Calgary connection: CAWST is based in Calgary and supports practitioners working in other communities and countries.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Start with the water: different problems require different treatment decisions.
2. Understand the filter: biosand resources describe a sand bed and a biologically active layer.
3. Teach operation: a device’s performance depends on appropriate use and maintenance.
4. Verify the outcome: clearer-looking water is not proof that it is safe to drink.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Use a paper filter cross-section and fictional observations of appearance before and after treatment. Separate what students can see from claims that would require testing.
**Discuss:** Why can a technically sound device still fail to solve a community’s problem?
**Teacher note:** This is a water-purification and education example, not a build-your-own drinking-water filter. CAWST is Calgary-based; its international project locations are separate from that local connection.

## Question

Why might revenue and profit change at different rates?

Growing sales do not tell you by themselves how a business’s result has changed.

## About the organization

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Identify comparable periods: use statements with consistent scope and definitions.
2. Separate revenue and expenses: a change in either can affect the result.
3. Calculate the relationship: compare the direction and size of changes, not only the largest headline number.
4. Explain the limits: a simplified comparison cannot replace the complete financial statements and notes.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Use fictional figures: revenue 100 then 120, expenses 80 then 105. Calculate the resulting profit and percentage changes, then explain why revenue growth alone gives an incomplete picture.
**Discuss:** How can revenue rise while profit falls?

## Question

How do occupancy, rent and operating costs change a housing business?

A housing business has to understand occupancy and costs together.

## About the organization

Boardwalk operates rental communities, including Calgary properties, and reports on its wider portfolio.

Who it serves: Residents and teams operating rental communities.

Calgary connection: Boardwalk lists rental communities in Calgary. Its group reports cover a wider portfolio.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Define the scope: a single property and a national portfolio are different units of analysis.
2. Track operating inputs: occupied units, rents and expenses influence property results.
3. Compare consistently: distinguish property operating measures from accounting profit and cash flow.
4. Interpret the change: explain what the calculation leaves out before drawing a conclusion.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** For a fictional property, compare 90 and 95 occupied units at the same monthly rent with different operating costs. Calculate a simplified property operating surplus and list omitted costs.
**Discuss:** Why might higher occupancy fail to produce the expected improvement in the overall business result?
**Teacher note:** The worksheet is a simplified property model, not reported NOI, accounting profit or cash flow. Group financial statements are not Calgary-only. FIN3060 requires FIN3040.

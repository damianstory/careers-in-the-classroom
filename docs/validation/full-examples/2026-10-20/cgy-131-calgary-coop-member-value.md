# Calgary Co-op
CGY-131-FULL · version 1 · **final copy pending** · proposal approved 20 October 2026

## Question

How can a member-owned retailer decide how much value to return to members?

A member-owned business has to consider today’s benefits and tomorrow’s ability to operate.

## About the organization

Calgary Co-op operates member-owned retail businesses and publishes summaries of its financial results and member returns.

Who it serves: Members and customers using its retail services.

Calgary connection: Calgary Co-op operates member-owned retail services in Calgary and surrounding communities.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Identify the value: members receive products, services and membership-related benefits.
2. Account for operations: revenue must support the costs of providing those services.
3. Consider future needs: investment and financial resilience compete with immediate uses of funds.
4. Explain the decision: compare alternatives in terms of customer benefits and the venture’s continuing capacity.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Allocate a fictional 100-unit surplus among member benefits, reinvestment and reserves. Explain the customer benefit and trade-off of each choice; this is not Co-op’s distribution formula.
**Discuss:** Can the choice with the largest immediate payment create less value over time?
**Teacher note:** Use the public results summary as organizational context. Do not imply access to member-restricted statements or equate sales with distributable surplus. All exercise amounts are invented.

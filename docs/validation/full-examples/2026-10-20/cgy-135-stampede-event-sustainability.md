# Calgary Stampede
CGY-135-FULL · version 1 · **final copy pending** · proposal approved 20 October 2026

## Question

How can a major event serve visitors and remain financially sustainable?

A successful event has to offer visitors something worthwhile and cover the work required to deliver it.

## About the organization

Calgary Stampede organizes events and activities with a major Calgary gathering at their centre.

Who it serves: Visitors, participants and communities engaging with its events.

Calgary connection: Calgary Stampede’s events and gathering spaces provide a direct Calgary connection.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Define the experience: identify what different visitors value.
2. Plan delivery: people, facilities and services create the experience and generate costs.
3. Compare scenarios: attendance and spending assumptions affect a budget.
4. Review the trade-offs: an affordable ticket, a better experience and financial resilience may pull in different directions.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Build a fictional one-day event budget with ticket income, a fixed venue cost and a per-visitor service cost. Test lower attendance and explain one adjustment.
**Discuss:** Which costs change when attendance changes, and which remain?
**Teacher note:** All budget figures are invented. Regional economic impact is not organizational revenue. No financial-performance claim is drawn from an unreadable annual-report viewer.

## Question

How can changing one company record update the relationships that depend on it?

A change to one record can matter to many people and organizations connected to it.

## About the organization

Athennian provides entity-management software and has Calgary and Toronto office hubs.

Who it serves: Legal, finance and governance teams maintaining organization records.

Calgary connection: Athennian lists office hubs in Calgary and Toronto alongside a distributed team.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Distinguish entities: people, organizations and appointments are different record types.
2. Record relationships: a person can hold more than one appointment and an organization can have several connections.
3. Update carefully: stable identifiers help preserve the intended relationships.
4. Review the history: changes need context so users can understand what is current.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Design fictional Person, Company and Appointment tables. End one appointment without deleting the person or their other appointment.
**Discuss:** Why can deleting an entire record be the wrong way to describe a relationship that has ended?
**Teacher note:** The schema is an educational interpretation, not Athennian’s internal architecture or legal advice. Use invented people and organizations. Software/database preparation replaces the unrelated geomatics route.

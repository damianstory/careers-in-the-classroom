## Question

How can software keep the right client, matter and document connected?

A document is easier to find when its relationships are stored consistently.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Identify the records: distinguish a client, a matter and a document.
2. Give each record an identity: stable keys avoid relying only on similar-looking names.
3. Connect records: relationships associate each document with the relevant matter and client.
4. Check the result: test that updates and searches return the intended information.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Build three small fictional tables for clients, matters and documents. Add a second matter for one client, then explain why copying the client’s address into every document row creates problems.
**Discuss:** When should two records share a link instead of duplicating the same information?
**Teacher note:** The relational schema is our teaching model, not Clio’s database design. Use invented identities and documents. INF2070 expects prior flat-file database understanding despite having no formal prerequisite.

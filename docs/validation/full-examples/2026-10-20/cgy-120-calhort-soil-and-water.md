## Question

How does soil change the amount of water a garden needs?

The same amount of watering can behave differently in different soils.

## How it works

1. Look below the plant: soil characteristics influence movement and retention of water.
2. Compare conditions: drainage and moisture affect the plant’s environment.
3. Observe over time: one wet surface does not describe the whole root zone.
4. Adjust using evidence: connect observations to plant needs rather than a universal watering rule.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Compare equal volumes of two clean classroom soil samples using the same added water and collection time. Record drainage, identify uncontrolled variables and avoid generalizing to every garden.
**Discuss:** Why can watering more often make some growing problems worse?

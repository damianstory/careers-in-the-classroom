# Monogram Coffee
CGY-136-FULL · version 1 · **final copy pending** · proposal approved 20 October 2026

## Question

Why offer a subscription as well as a single purchase?

A subscription changes the customer’s decision and the business’s delivery routine.

## About the organization

Monogram roasts coffee in Calgary and sells both individual purchases and subscriptions.

Who it serves: Coffee customers choosing how often and how flexibly to buy.

Calgary connection: Monogram roasts coffee in Calgary and sells to customers beyond the city.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Compare customer needs: some buyers want a single purchase; others value a recurring delivery.
2. Describe the offer: quantity, timing and flexibility affect the customer benefit.
3. Plan fulfilment: recurring orders still require stock, preparation and delivery.
4. Evaluate the model: convenience does not prove retention or profitability; those need evidence.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Design two fictional customer profiles and choose a purchase model for each. Compare benefits and possible drawbacks, then list data needed to test the decision.
**Discuss:** When could a subscription be less useful to a customer than buying one item at a time?
**Teacher note:** The source documents adjustable subscriptions. No retention, acquisition-cost or profitability claim is made. Any forecast in class must be labelled hypothetical.

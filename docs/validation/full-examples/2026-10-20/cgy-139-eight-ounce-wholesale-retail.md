# Eight Ounce Coffee
CGY-139-FULL · version 1 · **final copy pending** · proposal approved 20 October 2026

## Question

Why would a business sell to both cafés and individual customers?

Selling the same equipment to a café and a home customer can require different work.

## About the organization

Eight Ounce Coffee distributes coffee equipment and operates a Calgary warehouse and showroom.

Who it serves: Cafés, retailers and individual coffee-equipment customers.

Calgary connection: Eight Ounce Coffee operates a warehouse and showroom in Calgary and serves customers in a wider market.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Distinguish customers: a business buyer and a household may have different quantities and service needs.
2. Select products: purchasing decisions must fit customers and available supply.
3. Coordinate orders: inventory and fulfilment connect the offer to the delivered product.
4. Compare channels: assess customer benefits and operational demands without assuming one channel is always more profitable.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Compare fictional café and household orders. Identify differences in quantity, support and fulfilment needs, then recommend one process change with an explicit trade-off.
**Discuss:** Why might a company choose to serve both business customers and individuals?
**Teacher note:** The source describes in-house Calgary fulfilment. That does not prove it is cheaper than a third-party provider. No actual margins, supplier terms or sales volumes are inferred.

## Question

Why can a pump work well at one flow rate and poorly at another?

Moving liquid requires energy, and useful output changes with operating conditions.

## About the organization

Westpower supplies and services pumps and other rotating equipment.

Who it serves: Industrial operators maintaining and improving equipment performance.

Calgary connection: Westpower lists Calgary operations within its rotating-equipment business.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Define the duty: specify the required movement of liquid and operating conditions.
2. Compare performance: the same pump does not behave identically at every operating point.
3. Inspect the equipment: mechanical condition can affect reliability and performance.
4. Test the change: measurements are needed to judge whether a repair or adjustment helped.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Compare two fictional systems lifting equal water masses through the same height. Calculate useful gravitational energy and compare it with hypothetical input energy.
**Discuss:** Why can a pump that turns normally still fail to do the required job efficiently?

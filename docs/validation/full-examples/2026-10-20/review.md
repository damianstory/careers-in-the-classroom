Each final draft and Discover proposal starts pending. Review choices are approve v1, revise v1 or hold v1. After final-copy approval and an implementation instruction, generate the reviewed illustrations, adapt the data to the current layout, integrate Discover, validate locally and present the result before any push or publication. The three conditions above remain explicit until resolved; approval must not silently erase them.

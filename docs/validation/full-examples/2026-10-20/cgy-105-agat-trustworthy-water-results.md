## Question

How do you know a water-test result can be trusted?

A number becomes useful when you can explain how the sample and measurement were checked.

## About the organization

AGAT provides laboratory testing and analytical services.

Who it serves: Organizations that need evidence about materials and environmental samples.

Calgary connection: AGAT lists laboratory operations in Calgary. Services and accreditation must be checked for the particular site and method.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Identify the sample: record where it came from and what question the test should answer.
2. Prepare consistently: a suitable method controls the steps between collection and measurement.
3. Check the measurement: reference materials, blanks or repeat measurements can reveal different problems.
4. Report the limits: a result needs units, context and an explanation of what the method can establish.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Use fictional concentration results for a blank, a reference and two sample repeats. Ask which result needs investigation before reporting and why.
**Discuss:** Can two very similar results both be wrong?
**Teacher note:** The exercise is a generic analytical-quality model. It does not identify a particular accredited Calgary assay, detection limit or compliance result. Accreditation claims must stay method- and site-specific.

## Question

How can measurements help decide whether a training plan is working?

A training decision is stronger when a measurement answers a specific question.

## About the organization

Canadian Sport Institute Alberta supports athletes and coaches through sport-science services.

Who it serves: Athletes and coaches planning and evaluating training.

Calgary connection: Canadian Sport Institute Alberta lists Calgary operations at WinSport and the University of Calgary; the example focuses on its sport-science work.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Ask a focused question: decide which response a training change should affect.
2. Measure consistently: testing conditions influence the meaning of a result.
3. Compare over time: a pattern across observations can be more informative than one personal best.
4. Interpret together: sport scientists and coaches consider the evidence alongside the athlete’s circumstances.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Graph fictional recovery measurements from two sessions. Identify a possible change and at least two alternative explanations before making a conclusion.
**Discuss:** What would make a fair before-and-after comparison of a training plan?

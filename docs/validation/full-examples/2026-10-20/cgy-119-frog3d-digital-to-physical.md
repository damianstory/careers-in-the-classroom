## Question

How can a digital shape become a physical object without losing its dimensions?

A digital shape needs dimensions and a workable manufacturing plan before it becomes an object.

## How it works

1. Define the shape: a digital model describes dimensions and geometry.
2. Plan the process: material, tool access and machine limits constrain fabrication.
3. Make a controlled cut: equipment follows a prepared manufacturing process.
4. Check the result: compare the physical part with the intended dimensions and revise the plan if needed.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Use a cardboard profile and a fictional tool-radius template. Identify one inside corner that the tool cannot reproduce and propose a design adjustment.
**Discuss:** Why can a shape be easy to draw but difficult to manufacture?

## Question

Why can identical solar panels produce different amounts of electricity?

Panel rating is only one part of the story of electricity production.

## How it works

1. Identify the input: sunlight available at the panel changes with conditions.
2. Consider the arrangement: orientation and shading affect exposure.
3. Convert energy: the system turns some incoming solar energy into usable electrical energy.
4. Compare like with like: distinguish instantaneous power from energy accumulated over time.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Compare fictional daily energy totals from two equal-sized systems with different shading. List what else must be known before judging which design is better.
**Discuss:** How could two panels with the same rating produce different monthly totals?

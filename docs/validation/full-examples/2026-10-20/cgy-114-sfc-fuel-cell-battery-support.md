## Question

How can a fuel cell keep a remote battery charged?

A remote power system needs a plan for when the battery loses charge.

## About the organization

SFC Energy Canada supplies and integrates power and control solutions, including fuel-cell systems.

Who it serves: Operators needing dependable power away from convenient grid connections.

Calgary connection: SFC Energy Canada lists a Calgary headquarters and local integration and service capabilities.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Estimate the demand: the equipment requires energy over time.
2. Store a reserve: a battery supplies the connected load.
3. Replenish the reserve: a fuel-cell system can charge the battery when its control conditions call for it.
4. Check the whole system: fuel supply, losses, operating conditions and maintenance affect useful service.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Use fictional daily load and charging values to draw an energy-flow diagram. Explain which values are energy amounts and which describe a rate.
**Discuss:** Why include a battery in a system that already has a fuel cell?

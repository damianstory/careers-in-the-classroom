## Question

How do you compare electricity prices recorded at different times and places?

Prices can only be compared fairly when their time, place and units are clear.

## About the organization

Who it serves: Teams analyzing electricity markets and operating energy assets.

Calgary connection: Arcobi identifies Calgary as its headquarters.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Define the comparison: select the market, period and measurement needed.
2. Organize the records: time, location and units belong with every value.
3. Prepare comparable data: missing records and different intervals can distort a comparison.
4. Ask a bounded question: a filtered result supports an analysis, not a guaranteed forecast.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Use invented market-price rows with two regions, two time intervals and one missing value. Design keys and filters, then identify a comparison that would be misleading.
**Discuss:** What can go wrong when two columns have the same label but different units?
**Teacher note:** Use fictional data; access to commercial datasets is not implied. Database work is the teaching focus, not investment advice or a claim of verified forecast accuracy.

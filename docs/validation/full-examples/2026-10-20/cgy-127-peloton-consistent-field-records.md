## Question

How can field measurements and office records stay consistent?

Measurements lose value when their location, units or identity become ambiguous.

## How it works

1. Identify the record: connect a measurement to a site and time.
2. Validate the fields: consistent units and required values help catch errors.
3. Relate the tables: site details and repeated measurements serve different purposes.
4. Review updates: people need to know which data changed and whether the result is usable.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Create fictional Site and Measurement tables. Find a duplicate key, missing unit and measurement linked to an unknown site; propose a check for each.
**Discuss:** Why is a plausible-looking number not enough to trust a record?

## Question

What can extra colours reveal that an ordinary photograph cannot?

An ordinary photograph combines information that a spectral sensor can separate.

## About the organization

ITRES develops airborne remote-sensing instruments and supports their use.

Who it serves: Teams collecting and interpreting spectral imagery.

Calgary connection: ITRES lists Calgary facilities for its remote-sensing business. Customer survey locations are separate from this local base.

## How it works

1. Separate wavelengths: sensors record information in different parts of the spectrum.
2. Check the instrument: calibration helps make measurements interpretable.
3. Connect image and place: processing links measurements with locations and checks data quality.
4. Compare signatures: differences across bands can support a question that a normal colour image cannot answer alone.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Compare invented reflectance curves for three surfaces across four bands. Choose useful bands for distinguishing them and explain why the answer is not a real material identification.
**Discuss:** Why does adding more measurements also create more work before a map can be trusted?

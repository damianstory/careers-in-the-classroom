## Question

How do measurements get back from equipment far from a wired network?

A remote measurement is useful only if it reaches the people or systems that need it.

## About the organization

Microhard develops wireless communication equipment for industrial applications.

Who it serves: Teams connecting equipment and monitoring systems.

Calgary connection: Microhard lists a Calgary address for its wireless-communications business.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Measure locally: a sensor produces information at the equipment.
2. Send a signal: radio equipment carries encoded information across a wireless link.
3. Check reception: distance, obstacles and interference can affect communication.
4. Use the data: the receiving system needs to know whether information arrived and whether it is current.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Draw three fictional radio routes around hills and buildings. Identify uncertainties and propose measurements to compare them; do not calculate an unsupported product range.

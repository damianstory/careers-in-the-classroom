# Engineered Power — Lithion Battery
CGY-118-FULL · version 1 · **final copy pending** · proposal approved 20 October 2026

## Question

How can a battery keep working under heat and vibration?

A battery’s chemistry must work inside a physical package that can survive its environment.

## About the organization

Engineered Power, part of Lithion, designs and manufactures primary lithium cells in Calgary.

Who it serves: Industrial users needing batteries suited to demanding conditions.

Calgary connection: Engineered Power describes primary lithium-cell design and manufacturing in Calgary.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Choose the application: temperature, vibration and energy needs set constraints.
2. Use electrochemistry: chemical reactions drive charge through an external circuit.
3. Design the package: materials and connections must function under the expected conditions.
4. Test against requirements: performance and physical reliability both matter.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Rank fictional battery designs against a set of application constraints. Explain why the highest room-temperature capacity is not automatically the best choice.
**Discuss:** What can a battery specification tell you, and what would still need testing?
**Teacher note:** These are primary cells, not a rechargeable-storage example. No opening, heating, charging or destructive battery testing is proposed. Keep the schematic generic rather than inventing proprietary chemistry.

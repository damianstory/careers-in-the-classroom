## Question

How can an animal-health product batch be made consistent?

Making the same product again requires more than repeating the ingredient list.

## About the organization

Solvet and Alberta Veterinary Laboratories develop and manufacture animal-health products.

Who it serves: Animal-health professionals and livestock producers.

Calgary connection: Solvet and Alberta Veterinary Laboratories have a Calgary base; the example describes company-wide manufacturing and quality work.

Company presence and company-wide work are independent claims. Do not present every role or project as Calgary-based.

## How it works

1. Define the specification: describe the intended product and measurable checks.
2. Control preparation: consistent quantities and recorded steps make batches comparable.
3. Check the batch: quality work examines whether the evidence meets the specification.
4. Keep traceability: records connect ingredients, preparation and checks so a problem can be investigated.

## Classroom use

**Try it:** Compare two fictional coloured-water mixtures prepared from different total volumes. Calculate concentration and explain why equal colour alone is not a complete quality check.
**Discuss:** What information would you need to investigate a batch that did not meet its specification?
**Teacher note:** This is a simplified concentration and documentation exercise, not Solvet’s manufacturing procedure. No veterinary doses, product recipes or professional qualification claims are included.

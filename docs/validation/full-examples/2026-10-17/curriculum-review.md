## Evidence used

- [Science 10 program](https://education.alberta.ca/media/159715/science10.pdf), 2005 updated 2014: inquiry, representation and technological systems provide context. A generic inquiry skill is not by itself a strong unit match.

The document explicitly says it is not the Program of Studies.
- [Chemistry 20–30 program](https://education.alberta.ca/media/159730/chem2030_07.pdf), updated 2014: solutions and concentration support the water/sampling context.
- [CBE Biology course description](https://eps.cbe.ab.ca/biology-20-30.aspx): ecosystem topic context only, not a substitute for the current provincial outcome text. A guessed provincial Biology PDF returned 404 and is not treated as evidence.

These are dated official/provider documents checked today, not a claim that no curriculum transition has occurred. Exact current outcome text should be confirmed with the adopting teacher before badges are published.

## Decisions before implementation

1. Broken Pencil and Joe Media: choose a specific science task or present them under an appropriate media/communication classification. Do not silently force a new discipline or claim broad media work is a Physics unit.
2. Cinnaroll and Calgary Italian Bakery: retain the measurement/process inquiry focus and distinguish it from a precise chemical-reaction outcome.
3. O2, WaterSMART and ISL: check the exact environmental/system outcome against the selected course; advanced engineering or planning is application context.
4. CPKC: time-distance graphs are the classroom anchor. The simplified activity must not be mistaken for railway safety rules.

The full text can be reviewed with these limitations visible. These decisions remain open until explicitly resolved.

## Resolved gaps and limits

The [curriculum resolution record](curriculum-resolution.json) cites official Alberta materials and states the bounds of each classroom connection. Broken Pencil uses an explicit equal-time motion activity; Joe Media uses the path of light through a camera before distinguishing capture from editing. ISL uses aquatic ecosystem evidence rather than an unsupported kinematics match. Both bakeries are cross-unit inquiry examples and receive no invented unit alignment. These are editorial applications of curriculum, not teacher endorsement.

Physics references are explicitly identified as Alberta performance standards, supported by the current information bulletin; they are not mislabelled as the Program of Studies. Biology, Chemistry and Science 10 references are the official programs. Current high-school curriculum was not replaced by the junior-high science field-testing changes.

CPKC’s direct controller route is now represented by historical employer training. Indexed original employer text described six months of training for selected applicants, but the posting is now unavailable and its Montreal metadata conflicted with Calgary in the body. The pathway discloses both limitations and does not promise a current intake, duration or admission requirements. SAIT Railway Conductor remains attached to conductor work, not controller qualification. Software education is labelled an adjacent route. No CPKC live job card was added.

The two approved cards are O2’s undated Junior Landscape Designer PDF (status unknown, historical role evidence) and Calgary Italian Bakery’s Skilled Commercial Bakers opportunity (employer page rechecked on 17 October). Job status and employer requirements remain distinct from editorial role summaries.

## Illustrations

All 28 served illustrations use the approved graphic-novel / limited-colour screenprint direction. Fictional workers and generic equipment represent the sourced work; they are not photographs, proprietary designs or measured results. There are no green image-caption bands. The static teaching diagrams have directional arrows and separate live explanations; image sizes preserve their aspect ratios and remain at or below 680px on desktop.

Exact prompts, original assets, two rejected intermediate images and visual reviews are documented in [the generation record](../../../../design/example-expansion-2026-10-17/generation-record.json). One missing arrow and one unwanted green strip were repaired before integration. Generated charts remain illustrative and are identified as such in the live copy. Accessible story-image descriptions were checked against the selected artwork.

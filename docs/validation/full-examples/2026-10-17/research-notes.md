# Research scope and source handling

Research date: 17 October 2026, America/Toronto. Some provider timestamps fall on 18 October UTC, still 17 October locally. Source publication dates remain independent of these capture dates.

Free original company/project sources, official program pages and same-day discovery snapshots were used first. Additional searches covered local project details, employer role evidence, provider programs, apprenticeship and curriculum. Repeated unsuccessful controller-training queries did not justify inventing a direct-entry route. The old agreement is labelled historical and the newer agreement announcement prevents treating its terms as current. No exhaustive job-market search is claimed.

A single Looot call recovered a difficult Wix page after free-reader failure and thin raw extraction. It supplied company-team context, not proprietary production detail. Pricing was inspected at $0.005, balance checked, and $0.015 (3x settlement ceiling) reserved under an exclusive ledger lock before the call. maxAttempts=1 and maxCostUsd=0.005 bounded the request. The actual charge was $0.005 and the entire reservation was settled; no provider retry or top-up.

Some direct originals returned 403 but were readable in the free web reader. These failures remain in source-captures.json rather than being relabelled successful snapshots. Company source descriptions, generic role explanations, teaching interpretations and illustrative numbers are kept distinct in every draft. The 2025 upload path of the bakery case study is not treated as its study date.

Generated images were not requested in this research stage; 28 image briefs are prepared for later production and review. No employer logo or project photograph has been copied into the app.

## What changed through research

- Calgary Italian Bakery now uses a documented **historical food-loss assessment**. Potential savings are not presented as achieved results. Cinnaroll remains the separate batch-consistency story.
- Integrated Sustainability focuses on **operating reliability**, and ISL on its documented **creek/terrain crossing**. RJC’s wording now says **manage moisture**, not simply keep all moisture out. These new headlines are part of the full-copy review.
- Entuitive and ISL have named Calgary projects; RJC and Trace have explicit local-team evidence. Client treatment plants are not silently relocated to Calgary.
- Broken Pencil’s production article supports storyboard and animation roles. Joe Media supports editing and colour work. Their **primary science-course placement remains a review issue**; neither should receive an automatic science badge.
- CPKC has a verified Calgary operations-centre connection. SAIT’s conductor route is relevant railway education, but **it is not a controller qualification**.

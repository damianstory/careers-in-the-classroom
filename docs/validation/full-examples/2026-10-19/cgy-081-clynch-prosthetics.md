## How it works

1. A practitioner discusses the person’s activities and measures the limb and movement needs.
2. Measurements and a cast/model help define a device that must fit this individual.
3. A trial fitting lets the team examine comfort, alignment and movement together.
4. The team revises the device and follows up; a good-looking object is not automatically a useful fit.

These steps are a sourced classroom explanation. They are not an employer operating procedure, an exact facility diagram or proof of measured outcomes.

## Classroom conversation

Why could a device that fits its measurements still need adjustment?

**Activity:** On paper, draw the support forces on a simplified artificial foot while a person is standing still. Explain which observations—fit, movement or comfort—the force diagram cannot supply.

**Teacher note:** The force diagram is a classroom model, not an analysis of a real patient or advice on fitting a prosthesis. Do not imply that technicians independently make clinical prescriptions.

### Prosthetic and orthotic technician

**Proposed role ID:** work-prosthetic-fabrication · new profile

Turn a clinical design into a carefully made device.



- **Shape:** Use a model and the prescribed design to form device components.
- **Assemble:** Join and finish parts while checking dimensions and workmanship.
- **Revise:** Work with the clinician to address fit or fabrication issues.

**Works with:** Prosthetists and orthotists, Other technicians.

**Classroom connection:** A design has to become a physical object whose shape and materials suit its purpose.

**Company link/evidence:** Employer describes prosthetists and on-site fabrication. Fabrication technician tasks are a sourced occupational interpretation, not a verified individual employee title.

**Preparation routes:** George Brown Polytechnic — Orthotic/Prosthetic Technician S102.

### Prosthetist

**Proposed role ID:** work-prosthetic-clinical · new profile

Assess a person’s needs and evaluate a prosthesis with them.



- **Assess:** Discuss goals and collect measurements for an individual design.
- **Fit:** Evaluate a trial device and its alignment with the person.
- **Follow up:** Use comfort, movement and clinical observations to guide changes.

**Works with:** Clients, Technicians, Rehabilitation teams.

**Classroom connection:** A useful solution is judged by evidence from the person using it, not by appearance alone.

**Company link/evidence:** Employer describes prosthetists and on-site fabrication. Fabrication technician tasks are a sourced occupational interpretation, not a verified individual employee title.

## How it works

1. Ground crews assess frozen contamination and the conditions affecting the aircraft.
2. Trained operators use the applicable deicing/anti-icing procedure and equipment.
3. Communication and inspections help establish what was done and what remains to be checked.
4. Changing conditions mean observations and the approved process must remain current before departure.

These steps are a sourced classroom explanation. They are not an employer operating procedure, an exact facility diagram or proof of measured outcomes.

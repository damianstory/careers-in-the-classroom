## How it works

1. A collected donation enters a controlled receiving and identification workflow.
2. Processing separates blood into useful components rather than treating it as one interchangeable liquid.
3. Testing, quality checks and records support the decisions about what may enter the supply chain.
4. Specified storage and distribution conditions preserve the product and its identity until it reaches the hospital workflow.

These steps are a sourced classroom explanation. They are not an employer operating procedure, an exact facility diagram or proof of measured outcomes.

## Classroom conversation

Why would a correctly processed component still be unusable if its identity or history were uncertain?

**Activity:** Use fictional labelled counters for red cells, plasma and platelets. Design a component-and-record tracking diagram and identify where a label mix-up would break confidence. Do not use real blood or make transfusion decisions.

**Teacher note:** The model omits clinical release rules and compatibility decisions. A component picture cannot determine whether it is suitable for a patient; testing and professional decisions remain essential.

### Blood-component production work

**Proposed role ID:** work-blood-production · new profile

Help turn collected donations into traceable blood components under controlled procedures.



- **Prepare:** Handle incoming products and follow the production workflow.
- **Track:** Keep identity and processing records linked to the product.
- **Store:** Maintain the prescribed handling and storage workflow.

**Works with:** Laboratory technologists, Quality teams, Distribution staff.

**Classroom connection:** Separating material is not enough; its identity and handling history must remain reliable.

**Company link/evidence:** Calgary facility source documents production and testing. Occupational profiles explain related work; the book does not establish a current vacancy or the exact qualifications of every current worker.

**Preparation routes:** SAIT — Medical Laboratory Assistant.

### Medical laboratory technologist

**Proposed role ID:** work-medical-laboratory · new profile

Perform laboratory testing within a clinical quality system.



- **Test:** Prepare samples and operate validated testing processes.
- **Check:** Examine controls and results for technical quality.
- **Document:** Record and communicate results within the laboratory’s professional scope.

**Works with:** Laboratory assistants, Quality staff, Clinical and production teams.

**Classroom connection:** A result is useful only when the method, controls and sample identity are trustworthy.

**Company link/evidence:** Calgary facility source documents production and testing. Occupational profiles explain related work; the book does not establish a current vacancy or the exact qualifications of every current worker.

**Preparation routes:** SAIT — Medical Laboratory Technology.

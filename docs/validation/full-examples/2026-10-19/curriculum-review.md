# Curriculum review — 19 October 2026

All mappings are editorial. Application extensions are explicit; no employer endorsements or invented exact curriculum outcomes. Physics uses performance standards, not the program of studies. Official PDF/text hashes are recorded in [curriculum-sources.json](curriculum-sources.json).

|---|---|---|

Westech and Marlow have no forced unit links. Their measurement/prototype activities use the Science 10 cross-unit inquiry/design skills. The malting example draws on living-cell processes; it is not a photosynthesis or fermentation lesson. The deicing example uses solution properties as an application; it does not supply operating procedures.

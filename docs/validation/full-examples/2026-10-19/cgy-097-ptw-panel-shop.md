## How it works

1. A control need is represented by drawings, component choices and intended signal paths.
2. Trades and technical teams assemble the panel and make the specified connections.
3. Quality checks and a factory acceptance test compare what was built with the intended behaviour.
4. Issues can be recorded and corrected before shipment, followed by the project’s later installation and commissioning work.

These steps are a sourced classroom explanation. They are not an employer operating procedure, an exact facility diagram or proof of measured outcomes.

### Instrumentation and controls

**Proposed role ID:** work-controls · existing shared profile

Keep a process measurable and responsive. Controls work connects sensors, decisions and actuators so the system can respond when conditions change.



- **Investigate:** Check whether sensors are reading reliably.
- **Test and compare:** Compare measurements with the intended operating range.
- **Explain and improve:** Test how the system responds to a change or fault.

**Works with:** Operators, electricians and process engineers.

**Classroom connection:** Feedback uses a measured result to inform the next action.

**Company link/evidence:** Dated 2023 articles establish panel construction, QA/QC and factory acceptance testing in Calgary. Trades/controls descriptions are related work, not a current staff list.

**Preparation routes:** SAIT — Instrumentation Engineering Technology.

### Quality and traceability

**Proposed role ID:** work-quality · existing shared profile

Make it possible to explain how a result or product was produced. Quality work checks records, methods and acceptance criteria so variation can be investigated rather than hidden.



- **Investigate:** Check that materials and samples have traceable identities.
- **Test and compare:** Compare recorded results with defined acceptance criteria.
- **Explain and improve:** Investigate deviations and document what changed.

**Works with:** Production, laboratory, engineering and customer teams.

**Classroom connection:** Repeated results need both measurements and records of the conditions that produced them.

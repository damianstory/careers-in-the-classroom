## How it works

1. A window must transmit useful light while limiting unwanted heat transfer and air leakage.
2. Glass, gaps and frame materials create different paths for energy transfer.
3. Glazing and frame features can reduce some transfer paths; they do not stop all heat movement.
4. Installation and seals connect the product to the wall, so the finished system matters as well as the glass.

These steps are a sourced classroom explanation. They are not an employer operating procedure, an exact facility diagram or proof of measured outcomes.

## Classroom conversation

Why could the same window product perform differently in two installations?

**Activity:** Compare two fictional building-envelope sketches: identical glazing but one poorly sealed frame. Mark possible heat/air paths and propose what controlled evidence would distinguish the effect of glass from the effect of installation.

**Teacher note:** Qualitative heat-transfer model, not a product-rating calculation. No Lux efficiency, energy saving or whole-home performance figure is claimed.

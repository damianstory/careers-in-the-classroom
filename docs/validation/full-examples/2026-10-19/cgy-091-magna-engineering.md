## How it works

1. Runoff arrives carrying water and material from the surrounding catchment.
2. Designed storage and flow paths manage how that water moves through the facility.
3. Wetland and biofiltration elements combine physical and biological processes; different pollutants behave differently.
4. Engineers document the design and use monitoring evidence to assess performance and maintenance needs.

These steps are a sourced classroom explanation. They are not an employer operating procedure, an exact facility diagram or proof of measured outcomes.

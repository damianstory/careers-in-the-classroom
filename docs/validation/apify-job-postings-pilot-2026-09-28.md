# Apify job-posting discovery pilot

Checked 28 September 2026. This is a research record, not a published vacancy feed or an app change. Six bounded Apify Actor runs used **$0.335 in run-reported usage** against a $5 maximum authorized for this test. The sum of the six per-run charge caps was $2.10. No site content, database, scheduler, or production service was changed. Run inputs, IDs, final charges, a sanitized candidate index, and source checks are in [the evidence folder](evidence/apify-pilot-2026-09-28/). The API credential and full third-party descriptions were not saved there.

## What was tested

The question was whether an Apify search could add useful job examples beyond the [manual GHGSat/Wilder research pilot](job-postings-research-pilot-2026-09-28.md): featured employer first, then related work in Calgary, then relevant work elsewhere. The comparison is about *editorially usable roles*, not the number of search hits. Both tested Actors ran with `LIMITED_PERMISSIONS` and a `maxTotalChargeUsd` run cap.

| Actor and query | Returned records | Final run usage (USD) | What the result means |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| HarvestAPI LinkedIn search, “emissions” near Calgary, last month | 20 | $0.021 | Location and topic were too loose: many records were Canada-wide, North America-wide, or unrelated. Not suitable for automatic display. |
| Fantastic.jobs career-site API, “Emissions” title in Calgary | 2 | $0.034 | Found distinct local field-monitoring and GHG accounting roles. |
| Career-site API, GHGSat organization | 5 | $0.070 | Four Workable language/version records correspond to the two English software roles already known; one London BambooHR role was new to this research. |
| Career-site API, “Biologist” or “Ecologist” title in Calgary | 12 | $0.154 | Useful local variety, but many roles concern aquatic studies, industrial assessment, or senior consulting rather than Wilder's species-recovery work. |
| Career-site API, “Remote Sensing” or “Methane” title in Canada | 3 | $0.046 | One agency repost and two distinct roles. One supposedly Canadian result has an original employer page that says **Denver, US**. |
| Career-site API, “Conservation” title in Alberta | 0 | $0.010 | No results under this narrow query; this does not establish that no such roles exist. |

**Total:** 42 returned records across the six runs, including duplicates and weak matches. The career-site Actor returned 22 records; the LinkedIn Actor returned 20. Counts should not be treated as coverage or verified vacancies. Pricing on the account's Free tier at test time was $0.001 per LinkedIn detail plus $0.001 start, and $0.012 per career-site job plus $0.01 start. Final run usage came from Apify's completed-run records, not an estimate from list prices. The initial synchronous responses understated job event charges; the final run records were checked again after completion.

| Wilder has no connected job-card collection. | A small reviewed Wilder collection could start with its historical employer role from the manual pilot, then show current nearby wildlife survey and habitat ecology work at Canacre and KCB. These are related occupations, not Wilder vacancies or confirmed owl-project partners. |
| Related employers and geography are manually inferred per story; there is no repeatable job-discovery feed or database. | Apify can produce a **candidate queue** with title, employer, location, source URL, captured date, and description for review. An editor would still choose role relevance, correct employer/location labels, check original application status, and write classroom summaries. |

An illustrative order would be: **featured employer**, then **related roles in the same city**, then **relevant roles elsewhere**, each with its own employer and source-status label. The search did not produce a current Wilder posting or a clearly relevant, verified Canadian methane remote-sensing role outside Calgary. The historical PDFs and partner-hosted postings from the manual pilot still supply different examples that these active-job searches missed. Apify also does not recover expired roles on its own; preserving source snapshots when first found remains important.

## Product decision

Apify looks useful as a **discovery layer**, especially the career-site Actor. It found one extra GHGSat careers channel and several local roles quickly at low cost. It is not yet dependable as the displayed source of truth: the LinkedIn search was noisy, one career-site result had the wrong country, and a strong emissions result could not be rechecked at its employer URL. A small, reviewed static selection is still enough for a first CitC example. A database becomes more useful when we repeatedly collect across stories, track first/last seen and source versions, record review decisions, and link the same role to multiple stories. That decision should follow collection volume and editorial workflow, rather than the mere availability of an Apify API.

For a next **non-publishing** test, run a repeat search on another featured company and compare how many genuinely new, source-verified role types survive review. Do not present raw Actor output as live jobs.

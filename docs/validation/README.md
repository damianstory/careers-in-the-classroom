# Careers in the Classroom — Calgary validation pack

Prepared 24 September 2026. Research and discussion materials, not a launched service. No outreach has been sent or recurring automation enabled.

## Purpose

Help Alberta high school science educators connect teaching to real organizations, problems, roles, and possible pathways, beginning in Calgary. Build useful school and organization relationships for TKS without requiring referrals. Validate classroom use and repeat interest, alongside sustainable maintenance.

## Agreed experience

Teachers can search by course/topic or explore their city. A complete example is visible before free signup unlocks additional results. Include fully developed public examples. Signup captures school and board/authority and encourages a school email. Save interests and offer monthly examples and opportunities; do not infer unit sequence from course codes.

Display results on the site; email research follow-up is a fallback. Short explanations and discussion prompts are included; slides and other classroom formats require validation. A visible request-a-speaker route triggers just-in-time research and reviewed outreach, not a promise that a listed person is available.

An organization contribution route accepts corrections, suggested organizations, practitioner offers, resources, and potential support. Include provincial/national sector networks. Keep contributions and sponsorship transparent and rank for educational relevance.

## News as an input

Add a news-discovery workflow feeding topic searches and a monthly digest. No separate public feed is assumed. Accept both monitored sources and links Damian or educators supply. Distinguish a new announcement, demonstrated result, early research, and an older example newly discovered by us. A story outside Calgary can be valuable if its location and classroom connection are explicit.

## Still to validate

Classroom usefulness; teacher trust; best presentation format; signup friction; monthly cadence; willingness to request practitioners; maintenance effort; research costs; source coverage across science topics. Initial samples are deliberately small and do not establish full curriculum coverage. Technology/provider selection remains open.

Suggested next activity: show these examples to 5–6 educators spanning science disciplines, using the interview guide. These sample size and test thresholds are proposals, not established evidence or commitments.

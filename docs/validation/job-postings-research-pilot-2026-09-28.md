# Job-posting research pilot: GHGSat and Wilder Institute

Research checked 28 September 2026. This is a research and product-decision record, not published CitC content or a vacancy feed. No app data, database, scheduled job, or paid collection was changed. Sources below are working evidence; classroom interpretations still need editorial review. Six source PDFs, the ABMI page, and the GHGSat public feed were preserved locally with retrieval timestamps and hashes in [the evidence manifest](evidence/job-postings-2026-09-28/manifest.json). The IUCN page blocked direct archival; its live URL and key facts remain in this memo.

## Question and method

Would a small search across the featured employer, nearby related employers, and broader employers produce better role examples than the four postings currently saved for GHGSat? Two classroom contexts were sampled: GHGSat's methane-measurement story and Wilder Institute's burrowing-owl conservation story. Searches followed role terms, employer and partner pages, historical employer PDFs, an archived job-board index, and nearby organizations. A posting was counted as a *candidate* only when its title, employer, substantive responsibilities, location, and source could be inspected. Counts are a convenience for this sample, not estimates of market coverage.

## What the discovery routes taught us

- **Official or partner-hosted old postings were more productive than Wayback in this sample.** Seven of the nine candidate role types are in employer PDFs; IUCN retained the Wilder role after Jobvite removed it. These documents can carry more complete responsibilities than a scraped title.
- **Wayback is worth a fallback check, but coverage is source-specific.** A CDX query under GHGSat's Workable careers URL returned archived individual job URLs. One April 2025 snapshot yielded a “Front-end Developer — SPECTRA” title but no usable responsibilities in the retrieved HTML. Exact-url checks on other sites timed out or were rate-limited, and a Wilder category query returned no captures. This is a small diagnostic, not a general recovery-rate estimate. [CDX documentation](https://github.com/internetarchive/wayback/blob/master/wayback-cdx-server/README.md); [Wayback limitations](https://archivesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004651732-Using-The-Wayback-Machine).
- **Location needs its own evidence.** Highwood explicitly says Calgary. AIWC's hospital is near Calgary in Madden. ABMI allows Calgary or Edmonton office attendance while welcoming Alberta applicants. fRI's work is staged from Hinton; OSS is based in Penticton. Employer location cannot substitute for job location.
- **Status needs dated checks and explicit deadlines.** ABMI is the clearest counterexample to trusting a page's “Open” label. The retained employer PDFs are valuable historical records, not live opportunities.
- **Relevance should be explained, not inferred from a shared word.** A Wilder Conservation Officer is company-specific yet does not establish burrowing-owl project work. OSS names burrowing owls but is outside Alberta. The water/fish role is a weaker connection despite similar field methods.

## Net difference if reviewed results were incorporated

| Today | Supported direction from this pilot |
| --- | --- |
| GHGSat has four job cards, three from the company and one from a US space company; Wilder has no job-card collection. | GHGSat could gain a Calgary methane-measurement counterpart and a Canadian satellite-science counterpart. Wilder could gain its first carefully selected job examples across employer, nearby, Alberta, and broader tiers. |
| Each example manually lists job IDs, and a job can be selected within its linked role. | A reusable research collection could link a posting to one or more stories/roles with an explicit “why related” explanation. The classroom view should still curate a small set per role. |
| Card geography is one `local`/`broader` value. | Distinguish featured employer, Calgary job, nearby job, Alberta job, and wider example using actual work location. Allow city tiers to differ by story. |
| The app preserves a short adaptation and one status check. | Preserve source URL, observed text or source copy where permitted, retrieval date, stated posting/closing dates, review state, historical/current checks, and the classroom adaptation separately. |

The most persuasive product change is **breadth of actual work**: for GHGSat, satellite measurement can lead to local emissions interpretation and other Canadian remote-sensing science; for Wilder, a teacher can compare animal care, population monitoring, field work, habitat stewardship, and conservation planning. That variety should be presented as related work, not as evidence that the featured organization employs all those roles or that any historical role is hiring now.

## What this pilot did not establish

- It did not meet the proposed 12–20 useful-posting target. Nine inspectable candidates across eight employers were enough to expose source and relevance differences; adding weak results to reach a quota would distort the test.
- It did not run Apify. No `APIFY_TOKEN` is configured in this workspace, no callable Apify connector was available, and a paid Actor cap has not been finalized. Apify discovery yield, cost, and time saved remain **unmeasured**. Zero Apify charges were incurred.

A separate [bounded Apify follow-up](apify-job-postings-pilot-2026-09-28.md) was run later on 28 September. Its results and charges are recorded there; the limitations above describe this manual pilot only.
- It did not measure human minutes precisely or run a repeat refresh. It cannot establish cost per approved classroom example, site coverage, or ongoing maintenance effort.
- It did not save a complete copy of the IUCN page or approve any candidate for the app. The source URLs, dates, and key evidence above are preserved for review. An eventual collection process should capture source versions at first discovery, subject to source terms, before depending on them as durable records.

## Decision indicated by the evidence

Review a small publication set for each story, ranked first by role/classroom relevance and then by featured employer, Calgary, nearby, and wider location. Keep a larger internal research collection and a smaller reviewed classroom selection. Test Apify only when access and a spend cap are available, against the same source mix, recording unique *usable* postings beyond these nine rather than raw result count. A database becomes useful when repeat collection, source versions, review states, and links across multiple stories become routine; the current static records can still support a small editorial pilot.

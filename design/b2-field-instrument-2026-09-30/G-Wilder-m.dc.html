The lesson, in the real world

Supporting a vulnerable life stage is an intervention. Measuring recovery requires following what happens next.

  1. 1

    Wilder temporarily brings vulnerable young burrowing owls into care through their first winter.

  2. 2

    Animal-care teams provide daily care while veterinarians monitor health and body condition.

  3. 3

    Adults are released in breeding pairs in southeastern Alberta, with supplementary food and monitoring.

  4. 4

    To evaluate recovery, ask about survival and reproduction after release, over time and against a useful comparison. Counting released owls answers only part of that question.

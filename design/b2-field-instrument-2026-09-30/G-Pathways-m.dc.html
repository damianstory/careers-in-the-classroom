  • University of Calgary, Schulich School of Engineering

    University degree · Calgary

    Engineering Physics (BSc)

    Physics combined with electrical and mechanical engineering. One focus area covers aerospace, toward aircraft, rockets and satellites for near-earth orbit.

    Official program page (opens in a new tab)

    Why it connects to the work · our interpretation

    Satellite and instrument engineering

    The program page says graduates can develop new instruments, measurement techniques or prototype systems, and names satellites in its aerospace focus.

  • University of Alberta, Faculty of Science

    University degree · Edmonton

    Earth Sciences major (BSc)

    The Earth’s structure and evolution, and the atmosphere above it. Careers the page lists include meteorologist, global change researcher and research scientist.

    Official program page (opens in a new tab)

    Why it connects to the work · our interpretation

    Atmospheric science and remote sensing

    The program explicitly includes study of the atmosphere, the part of the Earth this measurement work is about.

  • University of Calgary, Schulich School of Engineering

    University degree · Calgary

    Geomatics Engineering (BSc)

    Collecting, modelling, analyzing and managing spatial data. Courses include remote sensing, satellite positioning and digital imaging.

    Official program page (opens in a new tab)

    Why it connects to the work · our interpretation

    Atmospheric science and remote sensing

    It teaches remote sensing, and the page asks how satellite images can be used to observe climate change.

    Software and data

    It is about analyzing and managing large amounts of spatial data, the kind satellite measurements produce.

  • SAIT

    Two-year diploma · Calgary

    Geomatics Engineering Technology

    Hands-on work with GIS, GPS and remote sensing to map and interpret the environment, including a remote sensing course.

    Official program page (opens in a new tab)

    Why it connects to the work · our interpretation

    Software and data

    Graduates work with mapping software and satellite and aerial data, which is how emissions results are located and shown.

  • SAIT

    Two-year diploma · Calgary

    Business Administration – Marketing

    Business and marketing foundations, market research, communication and customer relationships.

    About this route

    An introductory route into business skills. GHGSat’s manager role asks for a bachelor’s degree and five or more years of experience; this diploma alone does not meet that requirement.

    Official program page (opens in a new tab)

    Why it connects to the work · our interpretation

    Business development and partnerships

    Learning to understand customer needs and communicate an offer provides a foundation for business development.

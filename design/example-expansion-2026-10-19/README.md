# 19 October example and Discover artwork

43 selected illustrations: 15 landscape story scenes, 15 static teaching diagrams and 13 portrait Discover cards. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool in the approved ink-and-halftone style. All people and equipment are fictional; images are not proof of a company employee, facility, measured result or project assignment.

Original PNGs, exact prompts and selected WebP paths are in `generation-record.json`. Five superseded teaching generations are retained beside their revised selections. The served assets total about 11.1 MB. Each landscape preserves its full aspect ratio; the existing UI caps it at 680 px. Portrait title text fits under the existing 27 percent guessing cover. No example captions or disclaimer strips were added.

Visual review checked all 43 images using the contact sheets and individual views where needed. Corrections removed the misleading calibration balance, moved the cheese end-state marker, removed unverified deicing-facility insets, distinguished blood-component storage and replaced wild-pollinator imagery with managed-colony observations. Human review remains pending on the local site.

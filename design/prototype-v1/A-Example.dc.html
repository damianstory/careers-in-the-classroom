01 — THE PLACE

Connected to the Calgary Zoo. Working out on the prairie.

Wilder Institute is connected to the Calgary Zoo. Its burrowing owl field program works in southeastern Alberta, not in Calgary itself.

02 — THE PROBLEM

Help the young ones through their first winter. Then let them go.

The program cares for vulnerable young owls through their first winter, then releases adult pairs and monitors them. It uses one life stage to try to help a whole population recover.

WHY IT MATCHES BIOLOGY 20 · POPULATION CHANGE

An intervention at one life stage, used to try to change a whole population.
